## Top 5 Things to Know Today

### U.S. military intercepts two Iranian attack drones headed toward Bahrain

U.S. Central Command said it shot down two Iranian attack drones headed toward Bahrain without injuries or damage. Iran's Revolutionary Guard claimed it struck a U.S. early-warning radar and separately said a tanker hit a mine in the Strait of Hormuz; neither Iranian claim was independently verified. Bahrain hosts the U.S. Navy's Fifth Fleet, so even an intercepted attack is a material escalation risk in a critical shipping corridor.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/5d73a23b5b8647d2515c35b267b36c08)

### Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO; Colette Kress becomes interim chief

Nvidia said co-founder Jensen Huang resigned effective immediately after reports about extramarital relationships, including one that raised U.S. national-security concerns. Longtime CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. Nvidia powers much of the global AI-computing market, so the abrupt leadership change creates governance and supply-chain uncertainty well beyond one company; the underlying espionage allegations remain under investigation, not proven in court.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/5403f152a601cf82ce939f1d24fffa0f) · [AP background and transition details](https://apnews.com/article/314714d463cf578cbb0dbd49de903107)

### Operation TorNado produces 354 arrests and $234 million in seizures

DOJ and Europol announced the largest action in the history of the Joint Criminal Opioid and Darknet Enforcement team. Authorities reported 354 arrests across the United States, Europe, Australia and New Zealand, plus seizures of 5.7 metric tons of drugs, 209 firearms and more than $234 million in cash and virtual currency. Individual criminal charges remain allegations unless proved, but the completed arrests and seizures are a major cross-border enforcement action.

[Official Justice Department release](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/international-law-enforcement-operation-targeting-opioid-and-fentanyl-traffickers-dark-web)

### Richard Glossip pleads guilty to a lesser accessory charge after nearly 30 years on death row

Glossip pleaded guilty to helping conceal evidence after the 1997 killing of motel owner Barry Van Treese; prosecutors dismissed the murder charge, and sentencing is set for December. In 2025, the Supreme Court reversed Glossip's conviction because prosecutors failed to correct false testimony from the state's key witness and sent the case back for a new trial. The plea resolves the murder retrial but does not admit that Glossip planned or carried out the killing; he could be released or receive up to 15 additional years.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/d202b2a66357897349aeff8672ef81fd) · [Official Supreme Court opinion](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/24pdf/22-7466_5h25.pdf) · [Official Supreme Court docket](https://www.supremecourt.gov/docket/docketfiles/html/public/22-7466.html)

The result keeps the governorship in Democratic hands and means Democrats will hold a majority of U.S. governorships, expanding the party's role in Medicaid, election administration, education and other state-led policy areas.

## Most Useful Development Today

### CMS extends the nationwide freeze on new hospice and home-health enrollments for six months

The moratoria continue to block initial Medicare enrollment and certain majority-ownership changes for new hospices and home health agencies. Existing enrolled providers may keep serving patients and, under the modified policy, may add practice locations. The practical point for beneficiaries is that current Medicare coverage and currently enrolled providers are not frozen; this is an enrollment-integrity action aimed at new operators. CMS says an October California operation revoked 145 providers associated with more than $703 million in Medicare and Medicaid payments.

[Official CMS announcement](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-extends-modifies-nationwide-freeze-new-medicare-hospice-home-health-enrollment-six-months-combat) · [Official hospice moratorium notice](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-23242.pdf) · [Official home-health moratorium notice](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-23241.pdf)

## Supreme Court & Legal

### Glossip plea replaces a murder retrial with sentencing on an accessory count

The state's case had depended on Justin Sneed, who admitted killing Barry Van Treese but testified that Glossip hired him. In Glossip v. Oklahoma, Glossip asked the Supreme Court to overturn his death judgment because prosecutors did not correct Sneed's false testimony about mental-health treatment. The Court granted merits review and, in 2025, reversed the judgment and remanded for a new trial; that ruling did not acquit Glossip. Thursday's plea dismisses the murder count and moves the case to sentencing in December.

## Policy & Benefits

### Hospice and home-health enrollment moratoria remain national, with one practical modification

CMS extended both freezes through the next six months because it says fraud indicators persist. New initial enrollments and some majority-ownership changes remain blocked nationwide, while existing providers may now add locations under their current enrollment. Beneficiaries do not need to change Medicare coverage because of this notice, but access could still vary locally if a provider is separately revoked or suspended.

[Official CMS announcement](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-extends-modifies-nationwide-freeze-new-medicare-hospice-home-health-enrollment-six-months-combat)

### FDA issues the 2026 voluntary standards for retail-food regulators

The updated standards give state, local, Tribal and territorial agencies a common framework for inspections, enforcement, staff training and continuous improvement. The 2026 edition adds definitions for risk-based inspection and intervention strategy. Adoption is voluntary, but enrolled jurisdictions may qualify for related federal funding; the standards do not themselves create a new federal rule for individual restaurants or grocery stores.

[Official FDA update](https://www.fda.gov/food/hfp-constituent-updates/fda-issues-2026-voluntary-national-retail-food-regulatory-program-standards)


## Science, Medicine & Public Health

### American Cancer Society now recommends regular PSA screening

ACS changed its 2010 approach from offering shared decision-making without a fixed schedule to recommending PSA testing every two years for average-risk men ages 50–69. It recommends annual screening from 45–69 for Black men and non-Black men with a strong family history. A confirmed elevated PSA should generally be followed by MRI and/or a validated biomarker before biopsy. The guideline still requires informed discussion because screening can produce false positives, overdiagnosis and treatment harms.

[Official ACS guideline summary](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/types/prostate-cancer/detection-diagnosis-staging/acs-recommendations.html) · [Official ACS release](https://pressroom.cancer.org/prostate-cancer-screening-guideline-update-2026)

### HHS funds ibogaine development work and a $20 million rapid-acting-treatment research network

ARPA-H awarded up to $4.9 million for regulatory, manufacturing and clinical-data groundwork on ibogaine as a possible PTSD treatment, and NIMH opened a $20 million funding opportunity covering rapid-acting agents such as ketamine, esketamine and psilocybin. The ibogaine awards do not fund patient treatment or authorize a clinical trial, and HHS expressly notes potentially life-threatening cardiac-rhythm risks.

[Official HHS announcement](https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/hhs-advances-ibogaine-ptsd-research-rapid-acting-mental-health-treatments.html)

### New Free Journal Articles

### [TROP2-targeting aptamer delivers a cancer drug in pancreatic-tumor models](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13754016/)

Science Advances published a peer-reviewed preclinical laboratory and animal study. Researchers first examined TROP2 in 106 human pancreatic-cancer tumor samples, then created an aptamer–drug conjugate called T17-M and tested it in cell cultures, cell-line and patient-derived xenografts, and immunocompetent mouse models. T17-M selectively entered TROP2-expressing cells, suppressed tumor growth and liver metastasis, and improved tumor control with PD-1 blockade in mice. Why it matters: the compact aptamer may offer an alternative delivery platform for a cancer with few targeted options. Major limitation: no patient received T17-M, so human safety, dosing and efficacy are unknown; animal-model benefit is not an available treatment.

[Free full text — PubMed Central](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13754016/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aee3585)
