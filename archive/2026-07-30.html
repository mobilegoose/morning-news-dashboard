2. Most Useful Development Today

What the Medicare rules mean

Medicare · Facility payments

New nursing-home and psychiatric-facility rates do not change your basic Medicare eligibility rules

CMS finalized fiscal-year 2027 payment updates of 2.4% for skilled nursing facilities and 2.3% for inpatient psychiatric facilities, effective October 1. These percentages change what Medicare pays participating facilities; they are not a 2.4% or 2.3% increase in an individual’s benefits, nor do the fact sheets change the existing skilled-nursing coverage test, day limits or beneficiary cost sharing. For a real stay, confirm that the facility is Medicare-certified and ask the plan or Medicare how the admission will be covered. CMS skilled-nursing final rule, July 29 · CMS psychiatric-facility final rule, July 29 · Medicare coverage guidance

Why it matters: Provider-payment headlines can sound like personal benefit changes. The practical question remains whether the stay, facility and services meet Medicare’s coverage rules.

BEA’s advance estimate put real GDP growth at a 1.5% annual rate, down from 2.1% in the first quarter. Consumer spending and investment increased, while lower government spending was a drag. This is an initial estimate and will be revised as fuller data arrive. BEA, July 30 · AP, July 30

Federal Reserve · Rates

Fed held rates; three policymakers wanted a hike

The Federal Open Market Committee kept the federal-funds target at 3.5%–3.75% in a 9–3 vote. The dissenters preferred a quarter-point increase. Chair Kevin Warsh emphasized the 2% inflation goal but gave little forward guidance, leaving future action dependent on incoming inflation and labor data. Federal Reserve statement, July 29 · Implementation note

5. Policy & Benefits

Medicare, disability and access

Medicare · Skilled nursing

CMS finalized a 2.4% skilled-nursing payment increase

CMS estimates the fiscal-year 2027 rule will increase aggregate Medicare payments to skilled nursing facilities by about $883 million. It also removes two COVID-19 vaccination reporting measures beginning with the fiscal-year 2028 quality program. The rule changes provider payment and reporting; it does not itself expand covered days or waive the eligibility requirements for a beneficiary’s stay. CMS, July 29

Medicare · Mental health facilities

Inpatient psychiatric rates rise 2.3%, with a new outlier-payment safeguard

CMS estimates a $60 million aggregate increase in fiscal-year 2027 payments. Beginning in fiscal 2028, an individual psychiatric facility with at least 50 stays will generally have outlier payments capped at 20% of its total payments; CMS also finalized standardized patient assessment and removed two quality measures. CMS, July 29

6. Science, Medicine & Public Health

Evidence and access checked

Duchenne muscular dystrophy · FDA advisory review

Panel said deramiocel’s evidence did not establish effectiveness

Deramiocel is an investigational allogeneic cardiac-derived cell therapy proposed every three months for cardiomyopathy in Duchenne muscular dystrophy. FDA’s review discussed a 106-participant randomized phase III study, a 20-participant phase II study and a small extension cohort; the committee concluded that the total evidence was not sufficient to establish effectiveness. Advisory votes are nonbinding, so this is neither approval nor a final rejection, and it does not make the therapy available. FDA meeting record · FDA evidence presentation · Full FDA meeting video

Evidence label: Regulatory advisory review of randomized and extension data; final FDA action remains pending.

7. AI Today

Infrastructure, products and science

European Union · Infrastructure

EU opened a €10 billion call for seven AI gigafactories

The European Commission says the public funding is intended to attract another €20 billion in private investment. The facilities would combine advanced processors, cloud systems, high-speed networking and energy-efficient data centers; applications close November 12, with selections expected in 2027. This is a funding call, not seven completed facilities. European Commission, July 30 · AP, July 30

GitHub · Service retirement

GitHub Models shuts down for all customers today

The playground, model catalog, inference API, bring-your-own-key endpoints and related interface are being removed, including for existing active customers. GitHub recommends moving model-access workloads to Microsoft Foundry or using GitHub Copilot for coding assistance. Teams with scripts calling GitHub Models should migrate rather than assume the endpoints will continue. GitHub notice

Microsoft · Adoption and spending

Microsoft reported $90 billion in quarterly revenue and 30 million paid Copilot seats

Revenue rose 18% year over year; Azure revenue grew 43%, and Microsoft said Microsoft 365 Copilot exceeded 30 million paid seats. The results show commercial AI adoption at scale, but they are company-reported financial and usage metrics—not an independent measure of productivity or model quality. Microsoft, July 29 · AP, July 30

DeepMind · Scientific AI

Google reassigned most of the original AlphaFold team

Google DeepMind confirmed that researchers moved to Gemini-based projects, other scientific work and Isomorphic Labs, while reporting says nearly a quarter of the original group left. AlphaFold itself and its scientific use continue; the development is an organizational shift, not withdrawal of the system or its database. The Next Web, July 29 · Financial Times original report — may be paywalled

8. Other High-Value Recent Developments

Worth knowing

Telegram · Platform accountability

Telegram faced new legal pressure in both Russia and Australia

Russia charged founder Pavel Durov with facilitating terrorism and placed him on an international wanted list, alleging that Ukrainian intelligence used the service to recruit saboteurs; Durov denies wrongdoing. Separately, Australia’s eSafety Commissioner filed a civil case alleging Telegram failed to remove notified extremist videos, with potential penalties up to A$54.6 million. These are allegations and enforcement actions, not findings of guilt. AP on Russia, July 30 · AP on Australia, July 30
