### New Free Journal Articles

**[Updated guidance on neurological disorders associated with HIV-1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13474671/)** — *Neurological Research and Practice*, online August 13, 2026. **Evidence label:** peer-reviewed clinical guideline. The German Society of Neurology synthesized diagnosis and treatment recommendations for HIV-associated neurocognitive disorder, distal sensory polyneuropathy and opportunistic CNS infections. It consolidates practical neurological care but is guidance, not a new treatment trial. Limitations include mixed underlying evidence and German practice context. [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13474671/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42596004/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1186/s42466-026-00519-y)

**[The lived experience and impact of post-SSRI sexual dysfunction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13474523/)** — *International Journal of Qualitative Studies on Health and Well-being*, online August 13, 2026. **Evidence label:** peer-reviewed qualitative human study. Semi-structured interviews with 10 UK participants identified professional dismissal, altered identity, emotional numbness and relationship loss. The study describes experience rather than prevalence or treatment. Its very small, mostly White British, self-selected sample limits generalizability. [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13474523/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42595701/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/17482631.2026.2717470)

**[The miR-99b-3p/BMP8A axis in poor healing of osteoporotic femoral neck fractures](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13473059/)** — *Cytotechnology*, online August 13, 2026. **Evidence label:** peer-reviewed retrospective cohort plus cell experiments. In 160 patients—71 with poor healing and 89 with normal healing—higher miR-99b-3p was associated with poor healing and lower BMP8A; an exploratory model produced an AUC of 0.859. The signal may support biomarker research, but the single cohort lacked external validation and the laboratory work does not establish clinical benefit or causation. [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13473059/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42602031/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10616-026-01048-x)
