## 2. Most Useful Development Today

### Medicare facility-payment updates are not personal benefit increases

CMS finalized fiscal-year 2027 payment updates of 2.4% for skilled nursing facilities and 2.3% for inpatient psychiatric facilities, effective October 1. Those percentages change what Medicare pays facilities; the fact sheets do not expand skilled-nursing covered days or change the existing beneficiary eligibility and cost-sharing rules. For a real stay, confirm that the facility is Medicare-certified and ask Medicare or your plan how the admission will be covered. [CMS skilled-nursing final rule](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/fiscal-year-2027-skilled-nursing-facility-prospective-payment-system-final-rule-cms-1843-f) · [CMS psychiatric-facility final rule](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/fiscal-year-2027-medicare-inpatient-psychiatric-facility-prospective-payment-system-final-rule-cms) · [Medicare coverage guidance](https://www.medicare.gov/coverage/skilled-nursing-facility-care)

### *Trump v. California* — pending emergency application

The administration asks the Court to pause injunctions blocking parts of a March executive order in 23 states and the District of Columbia. The order directs federal agencies to build citizenship lists and the Postal Service to restrict mail-ballot delivery; the states argue that the President is intruding on authority assigned to states and Congress. The Court has not granted or denied relief. Responses are due August 3, and the lower-court injunction remains in force meanwhile. ## 6. Science, Medicine & Public Health

### Deramiocel for Duchenne cardiomyopathy

The investigational donor-derived cardiac-cell therapy is proposed every three months. FDA reviewed a 106-participant randomized phase III study, a 20-participant phase II study and a small extension cohort. The committee found the total evidence insufficient to establish effectiveness. The vote is advisory; final FDA action remains pending, and the therapy is not made available by this vote. [FDA meeting record](https://www.fda.gov/advisory-committees/advisory-committee-calendar/cellular-tissue-and-gene-therapies-advisory-committee-july-29-2026-meeting-announcement-updated) · [FDA evidence presentation](https://www.fda.gov/media/193913/download) · [Full meeting video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SUNn6YcBhnw)
