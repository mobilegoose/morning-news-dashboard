## Top 5 Things to Know Today

### More than 20 million Medicare enrollees are due a one-time $90 payment

The White House says the payment will help offset Part B premiums and will reach most eligible people by direct deposit in early October, with paper checks for those without direct deposit. It is a one-time payment from the Medicare Improvement Fund, not a permanent premium reduction or a new monthly benefit.

[White House fact sheet](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/10/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-medicare-improvement-fund-payments-for-seniors/) · [Reuters (potentially limited)](https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-says-us-send-one-time-90-payment-20-million-seniors-enrolled-medicare-2026-10-03/)

### Federal forces reportedly retake Mekelle’s main airport as the Tigray conflict escalates

Multiple local, humanitarian and diplomatic sources say Ethiopian government forces regained Alula Aba Nega Airport from a TPLF-led rebel alliance and encircled the regional capital. Media access is restricted and neither side has formally confirmed control, but the reported shift is the most significant battlefield development since fighting resumed September 23.

Lithuania closed the airport for less than an hour and NATO aircraft responded after authorities detected a possible object entering from Belarus. ### Federal judge blocks border-barrier construction across Texas’ Big Bend region

U.S. District Judge Kathleen Cardone issued a preliminary injunction stopping ground-disturbing work in Big Bend National Park and surrounding areas while a lawsuit proceeds. She found the landowners and environmental plaintiffs likely to prevail on at least one claim and likely to suffer irreparable harm. The order pauses construction; it does not finally invalidate the projects.

[Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/10/02/texas-big-bend-border-wall-court-ruling-injunction/) · [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/56ea4f79b989a4c894e1f2256bc0b886)

### Florida’s dengue outbreak passes 250 locally acquired cases

State officials reported 252 locally acquired infections, mostly around Tampa, plus one death. Available state and federal records indicate this is the largest calendar-year dengue outbreak in the continental United States since at least the 1940s. Local emergencies and mosquito-control operations are in effect.

## Most Useful Development Today

### How to handle the $90 Medicare payment—and avoid scams

The administration says eligible people do not need to apply: payments should arrive automatically by direct deposit or check. Do not give banking details, a Medicare number or payment to an unsolicited caller claiming to release the money. If the payment does not appear, the White House directs beneficiaries to Social Security at 1-800-772-1213; Medicare coverage questions go to 1-800-MEDICARE.

[Official payment details](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/10/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-medicare-improvement-fund-payments-for-seniors/) · [Official Medicare contact page](https://www.medicare.gov/talk-to-someone)

## Supreme Court & Legal

### Preliminary injunction stops construction while the constitutional challenge proceeds

Landowners, Friends of the Ruidosa Church and the Center for Biological Diversity argue that DHS used statutory waivers to bypass environmental protections and property rights for five border projects. Judge Kathleen Cardone granted preliminary relief after finding likely success on at least one claim, irreparable harm and equities favoring a pause. That preserves the landscape during litigation; the government may appeal, and the merits remain undecided.

[Texas Tribune case report](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/10/02/texas-big-bend-border-wall-court-ruling-injunction/) · [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/56ea4f79b989a4c894e1f2256bc0b886)

### Judge temporarily protects universities’ foreign-donor identities

AAU asked the court to stop the Education Department from publishing donor and contract-counterparty identities that members say they supplied under six years of confidentiality assurances. Judge Tanya Chutkan issued a temporary restraining order, finding AAU likely to show the planned release was arbitrary and capricious. The order expires October 29 unless extended, does not cover 92 entities already published, and is not a final merits ruling.

[AAU order summary and filing](https://www.aau.edu/resource-library/u-s-district-court-judge-issues-temporary-restraining-order-blocking-ed-from-publishing-aau-members-foreign-donor-identities) · [Reuters (potentially limited)](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-judge-blocks-trump-administration-publishing-names-universities-foreign-2026-10-02/)

### Judge dismisses DOJ’s novel synagogue-protest lawsuit but allows an amended complaint

The Justice Department used the FACE Act—best known for protecting reproductive-health facilities and also covering houses of worship—to sue pro-Palestinian groups over a 2024 New Jersey synagogue protest. Judge Katharine Hayden ruled the complaint did not adequately allege force, threats or obstruction of religious worship. Dismissal without prejudice means DOJ has 30 days to amend; it is not a final bar to the case.

[Readable report](https://www.internazionale.it/ultime-notizie-reuters/2026/10/03/us-judge-dismisses-doj-lawsuit-that-used-abortion-clinic-access-law-against-protesters) · [Reuters (potentially limited)](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-judge-dismisses-doj-lawsuit-that-used-abortion-clinic-access-law-against-2026-10-03/)

## Science, Medicine & Public Health

### FDA approves pirtobrutinib for previously untreated CLL or SLL without 17p deletion

Approval covers adults with chronic lymphocytic leukemia or small lymphocytic lymphoma and no known 17p deletion. In a 282-patient randomized open-label trial, progression-free survival favored pirtobrutinib over bendamustine plus rituximab; overall-survival data remain immature. Serious adverse reactions occurred in 28%, and the label carries infection, bleeding, cytopenia, arrhythmia and other warnings.

[FDA approval details](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-pirtobrutinib-previously-untreated-chronic-lymphocytic-leukemia-or-small-lymphocytic)

### Tampa-area outbreak becomes a modern continental-U.S. record

Florida counted 252 locally acquired cases as of last week, exceeding its previous annual record and approaching Hawaii’s 2015–16 six-month total. Officials have not requested CDC field investigators but have received $800,000 in federal assistance. Residents in affected areas are advised to use EPA-registered repellent and remove standing water.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/dengue-virus-outbreak-florida-7f0bda2b0a1e7895e834010f38cedb1e) · [CDC prevention guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/dengue/prevention/index.html)

### FDA finds Cyclospora at the Mexican farm linked to a record U.S. outbreak

Inspectors detected the parasite in a wastewater tank and a drainage ditch at the Taylor Farms site tied to nearly 13,000 illnesses. ### New Free Journal Articles

#### [Clinical factors associated with discordant plasma p-tau217 and established Alzheimer disease biomarkers](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13632642/)

Neurology · online October 1, 2026 · peer-reviewed retrospective human cohort. Among 706 Mayo Clinic participants, 60 (8.5%) had plasma p-tau217 results that disagreed with amyloid PET or cerebrospinal-fluid biomarkers; discordance was associated with age, sleep apnea, clinical stage, body mass index, kidney function and thyroid disease. Why it matters: blood tests may need confirmatory testing in clinically discordant cases. Limitation: this selected research cohort and assay thresholds may not generalize, and the associations do not establish causes of false results.

[Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13632642/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42821845/)

#### [Digital health technologies for risk-factor management and secondary prevention after stroke](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13631877/)

Neurology: Clinical Practice · online October 1, 2026 · peer-reviewed systematic review and meta-analysis. Across 29 studies with 8,389 people after stroke or TIA, digital interventions were associated with a modest 5.8 mmHg reduction in systolic blood pressure, but heterogeneity was substantial and the prediction interval crossed no effect; lipid, weight and other outcomes were inconsistent. Why it matters: remote support may help blood-pressure control. Limitation: follow-up was usually short, participants often had mild impairment, and few studies could assess recurrent stroke or mortality.

[Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13631877/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42821840/)

#### [Advances in molecular pathobiology of primary central nervous system lymphoma](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13632146/)

Biomarker Research · online October 1, 2026 · peer-reviewed open-access narrative review. The authors synthesize molecular subtypes, liquid-biopsy and multi-omics diagnostics, and emerging approaches including BTK inhibitors, immunomodulators and CAR-T therapy. Why it matters: it maps a fast-moving route toward more individualized diagnosis and treatment for a Compassionate Allowances cancer. Limitation: this is a narrative review, not a new trial; many biomarkers and therapies remain early-stage or unvalidated for routine care.

[Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13632146/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42823715/)
