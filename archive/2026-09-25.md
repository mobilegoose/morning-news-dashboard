## 2. Most Useful Development Today

### What the FDA panel’s Galleri vote does—and does not—mean

**MOST USEFUL · CANCER SCREENING**

An FDA advisory panel voted 10–0 that the multi-cancer blood test is safe, 6–4 that it is effective, and 7–2 (with one abstention) that benefits outweigh risks for adults 50 and older. This is advice to the FDA, not approval. Galleri is intended to supplement—not replace—recommended cancer screening; the key unresolved issues include false results, downstream procedures and whether earlier detection improves survival.

[Associated Press explainer](https://apnews.com/article/grail-test-cancer-fda-dna-8fc5480be365b59c450043daa38cad30) · [FDA meeting page and webcast](https://www.fda.gov/advisory-committees/advisory-committee-calendar/september-23-2026-molecular-and-clinical-genetics-panel-medical-devices-advisory-committee-meeting)

### Survival disparities in HER2-low versus HER2-positive breast cancer

**NEW FREE JOURNAL ARTICLE · SYSTEMATIC REVIEW**

Frontiers in Oncology · Online September 10, 2026 · Peer-reviewed systematic review. Researchers screened 180 records from 536 identified and included 10 trials. The qualitative synthesis found distinct survival patterns shaped by hormone-receptor coexpression and targeted therapy, but the studies were too clinically and methodologically heterogeneous for a pooled estimate. Why it matters: it refines an important breast-cancer treatment category. Limitation: the non-pooled, heterogeneous evidence cannot establish one universal survival difference.

[Free full text at PubMed Central](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13600857/)

### Cognitive decline over time in myotonic dystrophy type 1

**NEW FREE JOURNAL ARTICLE · SYSTEMATIC REVIEW**

Frontiers in Neurology · Online September 10, 2026 · Peer-reviewed systematic review. Sixteen longitudinal studies with follow-up ranging from 1 to 20 years suggested stable baseline impairment in pediatric phenotypes but slow, domain-specific decline—especially visuoconstruction and processing speed—in adult and late-onset disease. Why it matters: cognition affects disability, independence and care planning. Limitation: varied cohorts, measures and follow-up periods limit certainty and comparability.

[Free full text at PubMed Central](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13600866/)
