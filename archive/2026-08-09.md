## 1. Top 5 Things to Know Today

### 1. Syria and Russia reset the future of Moscow's two Mediterranean bases

After 18 months of talks, Syria said the two governments finalized a memorandum covering Tartous and Hmeimim. Syria will take control of civilian areas, including Hmeimim airport and Tartous's commercial dock; military zones are to become joint training centers during a three-month transition. **Why it matters:** Russia retains a military foothold after Bashar Assad's fall, but Damascus regains commercial infrastructure and converts exclusive bases into a narrower joint arrangement. [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/2ade8e8a7da23eae4a90c8070f002a86) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/syria-russia-reach-deal-future-tartous-hmeimim-bases-after-18-months-talks-2026-08-09/)

### 2. Houthi attacks hit a Saudi refinery and Yemen's Mokha port

The Houthis claimed a drone strike on Saudi Aramco's Jazan refinery, where authorities extinguished a fire and reported no casualties. A separate missile-and-drone assault on government-held Mokha killed at least seven people, wounded 15 and damaged the Red Sea port. **Why it matters:** the attacks threaten energy and commercial infrastructure and risk reopening a wider phase of Yemen's war. [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/6c5aa0c5debcca3fa36316a8b0b67400) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/fire-extinguished-aramco-facility-jizan-saudi-arabia-says-2026-08-09/)

### 3. Overnight Russia-Ukraine attacks killed seven people

Ukrainian drones killed five people and wounded 24 in Russia's Belgorod region; Russian attacks killed two people and wounded at least 21 in Ukraine, according to local authorities cited by AP. **Why it matters:** civilians on both sides remain exposed as long-range strikes expand, even while each government emphasizes military and energy targets. [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/4f1dd5120278884bf36ce85efe84e5d9)

### 4. Congo cleared a quarantined river boat of suspected Ebola cases

Health authorities said testing found no Ebola among more than 200 passengers isolated near Kinshasa after a former passenger died with Ebola-like symptoms; the boat was decontaminated. **Why it matters:** this removes one feared route into the capital, while the broader outbreak remains severe and contact tracing incomplete. [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/e992469947e275eb8fa27d2fe7ebd1b3)

### 5. A judge preserved the VA's largest union contract

**Recent fallback — August 7.** U.S. District Judge Melissa DuBose enforced an earlier injunction and blocked the Department of Veterans Affairs' third attempt to end its agreement with the American Federation of Government Employees, which covers roughly 320,000 workers. **Why it matters:** collective-bargaining rights remain in place while the lawsuit and the government's appeals continue; this is interim relief, not a final merits judgment. [Readable GovExec report](https://www.govexec.com/workforce/2026/08/judge-rejects-vas-third-attempt-cancel-union-contracts/415293/) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-judge-blocks-trump-administrations-third-attempt-cancel-va-union-contract-2026-08-07/)

### New Free Journal Articles

#### [Long-term angiogenic and thromboinflammatory signatures in post-COVID-19 syndrome](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13451242/)

*Angiogenesis* · electronically published August 7, 2026 · **Peer-reviewed observational biomarker cohort.** Investigators followed 32 adults hospitalized with severe or critical COVID-19 in Mexico City for 3.5 years, comparing paired acute and follow-up plasma. Fatigue, dyspnea and concentration problems remained common, and several angiogenic, inflammatory and coagulation measures stayed abnormal. The study supports an association between severe-COVID survivorship and persistent vascular signatures; it does not prove that the biomarkers cause symptoms or establish a treatment. **Why it matters:** it adds unusually long follow-up to Long COVID mechanism research. **Material limitation:** small, single-center cohort, 75% men, high obesity prevalence and no matched mild-COVID or uninfected control group. [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13451242/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42568003/)

#### [Locoregional and systemic adoptive cellular therapies for pediatric brain tumors](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13451245/)

*Neurosurgical Review* · electronically published August 7, 2026 · **Peer-reviewed systematic review of preclinical and early clinical evidence.** The authors screened 221 unique records and extracted 34 studies of CAR-T, TCR-engineered T-cell and NK-cell strategies. Preclinical activity and early human feasibility signals were strongest for several antigen targets and intracranial delivery approaches, but clinical programs were generally small and early phase. **Why it matters:** pediatric brain tumors include severe, often CAL-relevant conditions with major unmet treatment need. **Material limitation:** heterogeneous studies, substantial preclinical evidence, small single-center clinical cohorts and no basis yet to infer established efficacy. [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13451245/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42566099/)

#### [Frontotemporal dementia with right temporal predominance: a clinical comparison with left-predominant FTD](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13451453/)

*Journal of Neurology* · electronically published August 7, 2026 · **Peer-reviewed retrospective comparative study.** Researchers compared 51 clinic patients whose imaging showed greatest atrophy in the right or left anterior temporal lobe. Right-predominant cases more often showed difficulty recognizing people, impaired decisions, preoccupations, disinhibition and loss of empathy, but symptom overlap was substantial and group differences narrowed as disease progressed. **Why it matters:** it cautions against classifying disability from imaging laterality alone. **Material limitation:** retrospective specialist-clinic sample, modest diagnostic sensitivity and specificity, and no independent validation cohort. [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13451453/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42566133/)
