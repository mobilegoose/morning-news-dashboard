## 6. Science, Medicine & Public Health

### New Free Journal Articles

1. **[Remote Patient Monitoring Adoption for Hypertension Management Among Medicare Beneficiaries](<https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2852336>).** *JAMA Network Open*, online July 31, 2026. Observational matched cohort, n=281,620. Switching from fee-for-service to Medicare Advantage was associated with lower remote-monitoring adoption, more clinician loss and more hypertension-related acute care. This does not prove causation. It matters for Medicare continuity policy. Limitation: residual confounding and plan-type proxies.
2. **[Neighborhood Environmental Interventions and Opioid Overdose Rates](<https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2852333>).** *JAMA Network Open*, online July 31, 2026. Quasi-experimental nonrandomized trial across 1,667 Philadelphia blocks. Combined interventions were associated with 6.6% fewer fatal overdoses; abandoned-house remediation showed the clearest association. Limitation: one neighborhood, nonrandomized assignment and possible unmeasured confounding.
3. **[Probiotic-Associated Invasive Infection Among Preterm Infants](<https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2852339>).** *JAMA Network Open*, online July 31, 2026. Retrospective cohort, n=107,505. Supplementation was associated with an absolute invasive-infection increase of 0.04%, about one per 2,500 supplemented infants. The event was rare and must be weighed against known benefits. Limitation: retrospective data and no genomic proof of causation.
