1. Top 5 Things to Know Today

Ranked by public importance

1 · U.S. economy

Payrolls fell by 23,000 in July; unemployment held at 4.1%

BLS said both measures changed little statistically, but revisions cut May and June payrolls by a combined 103,000. Participation fell to 61.4%, its lowest in nearly five and a half years.

Why it mattersThe combination signals a softer labor market, though one month’s estimate is not a recession verdict.

BLS reportGuardian

2 · Immigration

Trump signed orders on birth tourism and citizenship

One directs rules or guidance to deny entry when a nonimmigrant’s primary purpose is giving birth; another revives birthright-citizenship restrictions after June’s Supreme Court ruling rejected the earlier effort.

Why it mattersImplementation and litigation will determine practical reach; the orders do not erase citizenship immediately.

Birth-tourism orderCitizenship orderAP explainer

3 · Media policy

FCC voted 2–1 to end the 39% national television-ownership cap

The agency substituted deal-by-deal public-interest review. The dissent says Congress set the cap and the FCC lacks authority to remove it.

Why it mattersThe vote could enable larger broadcast combinations, but legal challenges are likely.

FCCAxios

4 · World security

Saudi Arabia, Turkey and Pakistan signed a mutual-defense pact

The Mecca agreement treats an attack on one country as an attack on all three, linking three Sunni-majority U.S. partners during the Iran war.

Why it mattersIt materially changes regional security commitments as Saudi Arabia warns of possible attacks.

Associated Press

5 · Public health

FDA accelerated approval of a viral immunotherapy for advanced melanoma

Tudriqev with nivolumab is for unresectable advanced cutaneous melanoma that progressed after anti-PD-1 treatment. In a single-arm trial, 24% of 91 evaluable patients responded.

Why it mattersIt adds an option for a hard-to-treat group, but confirmatory evidence is required.

FDA
