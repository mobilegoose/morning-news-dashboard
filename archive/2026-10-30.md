## Top 5 Things to Know Today

### Judge blocks the administration's narrower 'medically frail' exemption

U.S. District Judge Richard Stearns vacated the part of a CMS rule that required people with serious medical conditions to show their condition also significantly impairs their ability to satisfy Medicaid's new work requirement. The ruling, in a suit by 25 states and Washington, D.C., preserves the statutory medical-frailty categories and grants the plaintiff states a six-month enforcement buffer for initial eligibility errors. It does not erase the broader 80-hours-a-month requirement scheduled for January 1, 2027, and the judge upheld separate documentation limits.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/656719bd7c8358d9d5290cf54f81255b) · [Massachusetts attorney general](https://www.mass.gov/news/ag-campbell-celebrates-major-victory-blocking-implementation-of-unlawful-medicaid-work-requirements-for-medically-frail-individuals)

### U.S. economic growth accelerates to a 3.4% annual rate

The Commerce Department's advance estimate says real GDP grew at a 3.4% annualized rate in the third quarter, up from 2.2% in the second. Consumer spending, investment and government spending rose while exports fell; the quarterly personal-consumption price index increased 2.1% and the core measure 2.5%. The headline is solid growth, but it is an early estimate and will be revised as more complete data arrive.

[Bureau of Economic Analysis](https://www.bea.gov/index.php/news/2026/gdp-advance-estimate-3rd-quarter-2026) · [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/c32cfa8b85ae53ceb7b1cc557d3b7400)

### Israel's final count leaves every bloc short of a majority

Final results give Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's bloc 53 seats, the main opposition 48 and Arab-led parties a record 19—none close to the 61 seats needed to govern. President Isaac Herzog will consult parties after certification and choose a lawmaker to try to form a coalition; the new Knesset is due to convene November 10. The result makes coalition bargaining, including the role of Arab-led parties, the decisive next stage.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/3a793c0d07fdfe515390524b849d8674)

### Aid convoy and 25 evacuation buses stage for occupied Oleshky

Ukraine says 25 buses with 750 seats, ambulances and relief supplies are ready for a localized ceasefire operation in Russian-occupied Oleshky after both sides reported demining complete. The pause is intended to evacuate civilians—especially older and disabled residents—and deliver aid through November 1. Preparation is concrete, but the operation's success and the number actually evacuated remain to be verified.

[Hromadske](https://hromadske.ua/en/war/273457-ukraine-prepares-25-buses-to-evacuate-civilians-from-russian-occupied-oleshky) · [Readable Reuters companion](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/russia-ukraine-say-oleshky-demining-operations-complete-humanitarian-operation-to-proceed-4985252)

### FDA proposes its first major infant-formula nutrient update in nearly 30 years

FDA proposed changing required ranges for iodine, manganese and vitamins A and D while reaffirming several other nutrient standards, the first phase of its comprehensive review since 1998. It also released product-testing data for contaminants. The proposal is open for comment through February 26, 2027 and does not immediately change products or prove that current formulas are unsafe.

## Most Useful Development Today

### FTC warns 25 plan marketers and lead generators about deceptive claims

FTC staff sent warning letters to companies advertising ACA coverage, limited-indemnity plans and medical-discount programs. The agency highlighted false claims about benefits, comprehensive coverage, costs and cash incentives. A warning letter is not an accusation or finding of wrongdoing; the practical takeaway is to verify that a product is actual major-medical insurance, confirm exclusions and total costs in writing, and use the official Marketplace when shopping for ACA coverage.

## U.S. & World

### Employer compensation costs rise 0.8% in the third quarter

The Labor Department's Employment Cost Index rose 0.8% from July through September: wages and salaries increased 0.7% and benefit costs 0.9%. Over 12 months, total compensation rose 3.4%, including 3.1% wage growth and 3.9% benefit-cost growth. The index is a broad measure of labor costs and helps distinguish pay pressure from noisier monthly earnings data.

[Bureau of Labor Statistics](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/eci_10302026.htm)

### U.S. cancels the G20 foreign-ministers 'family photo' after Russia dispute

The United States scrapped the posed group photo at the Atlanta meeting after several European diplomats objected to appearing with Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and threatened to walk out. The meeting itself is proceeding, but the cancellation is an unusually visible sign of the divisions over Ukraine, Iran, trade and maritime security that are limiting prospects for consensus.

[Associated Press readable copy](https://www.durangoherald.com/articles/associated-press/g20-diplomats-including-from-china-and-russia-face-deep-rifts-at-atlanta-meeting-scrap-photo/)


## Supreme Court & Legal

### DOJ expands its Alaska certification lawsuit and issues subpoenas

The Justice Department amended its complaint and subpoenaed state records tied to federal letters alleging noncitizens on Alaska's voter rolls. It asks a court to block certification if Republican Senator Dan Sullivan narrowly loses and ballots potentially confused by another candidate named Daniel J. Sullivan are decisive. No voter has been charged, Alaska calls the theory meritless, and the case has not established that any ineligible ballot was counted.

## Science, Medicine & Public Health

### New Free Journal Articles

### Blood-fragment test performs well in multicenter validation—but is not ready for routine screening

Advanced Science published a peer-reviewed diagnostic-model study of 1,260 participants from three Chinese centers. A plasma cell-free mitochondrial-DNA fragmentomics model distinguished esophageal cancer from non-cancer controls with AUCs of 0.9668–0.9762 across validation cohorts and reported 92.16% sensitivity for stage I disease at its selected cutoff; a second model classified major tumor subtypes. This matters for a Compassionate Allowances cancer because less-invasive earlier detection could eventually improve access to treatment. Important limitations: this was model development and validation, not a randomized trial or clinical-utility study; cohorts were all in China; performance depends on the chosen threshold and comparator mix; and the test is not an approved screening tool. Published online October 28, 2026.

[Free full text — PubMed Central](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13715668/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.78377)

OpenAI published a revised behavior specification centered on human intent, minimizing harm, honesty and transparency, agency and care. It adds guidance on context, oversight and preserving user control while retiring much detailed style guidance.
