**Wednesday, November 18, 2026 · Updated after 10:45 a.m. Eastern**

A concise, source-linked briefing focused on concrete developments, public impact and practical relevance.

## Top 5 Things to Know Today

### Mary Peltola wins Alaska's final Senate contest, giving Democrats a 56–44 majority

The Associated Press called Alaska's ranked-choice Senate race for Democrat Mary Peltola, who defeated two-term Republican Sen. Dan Sullivan. The result completes a nine-seat Democratic gain and makes Peltola the first Alaska Native elected to the Senate. DOJ's separate investigation and appeal concerning a similarly named candidate remain pending, but no court order blocked the vote count or AP's result call.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/f1fd4d243bf764ec46ff94db4c792953)

### The United States loses measles-elimination status after sustained transmission

An independent regional commission concluded that the same measles lineage circulated in the United States for at least 12 months, so PAHO accepted the finding that endemic transmission had been re-established. Mexico and Bolivia also lost elimination status. The designation does not mean measles is untreatable or present everywhere; it signals that vaccination and outbreak control failed to interrupt a continuous chain of transmission.

[PAHO official announcement](https://www.paho.org/en/news/17-11-2026-paho-urges-countries-step-efforts-stop-measles-outbreaks-and-move-towards) · [CDC joint statement](https://www.cdc.gov/media/releases/2026/loss-of-usmeasles-elimination-status.html)

### Appeals court blocks the Pentagon from expelling the transgender service members who sued

A divided D.C. Circuit panel denied the administration's request to postpone implementation of its June ruling while the government seeks Supreme Court review. The order protects the more than two dozen current service members who are plaintiffs from discharge; it does not open enlistment to new transgender recruits or suspend the policy nationwide. The government may still file a Supreme Court petition and request emergency relief.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/dcdb4c91f17e59c7c2dc5fb725d3b7e9)

### A classified NSA communications network suffers an outage during maintenance

The National Security Agency said scheduled maintenance caused temporary degradation of internal systems, including disruption to NSANet, while mission-critical capabilities remained operational. Officials said they had no evidence connecting this episode to the recent Microsoft, FAA, banking and airline disruptions or to malicious activity. The concrete concern is resilience: multiple legacy systems have failed during overlapping upgrades and security patching.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/22dc6be1bf1d1f5f86a7167f016e9526)

### Israel's parliament elects an opposition-backed speaker after Netanyahu's bloc loses its majority

The Knesset elected Matan Kahana speaker by 66–52, giving the parties seeking to replace Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu control of a central parliamentary office while Gadi Eisenkot tries to assemble a governing coalition. The vote is a concrete transfer of institutional power, not yet formation of a new government; Eisenkot still must secure a coalition within the remaining mandate period.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/342956b9475b69ba5181fd9057eb0b68)

CMS's CRUSH proposal would expand provider screening and ownership disclosures, require faster return of identified fee-for-service overpayments, strengthen Medicare Advantage and Medicaid program-integrity reporting, and increase Marketplace broker enforcement. Separately, CMS extended the nationwide moratoria on new Medicare hospice and home-health enrollments for six months beginning November 13; existing enrolled providers are not automatically terminated.

### HUD and Los Angeles's homelessness agency settle their funding dispute

Under the settlement, the Los Angeles Homeless Services Authority will return about $150 million and permanently stop receiving HUD funds by the end of 2027; Los Angeles County will assume the lead role for federal homelessness grants. HUD cites audit findings and alleged mismanagement, while LAHSA says it disputed the suspension as unlawful. The agreement resolves the litigation but creates an implementation risk for continuity of local services.

Procedurally, the plaintiffs now receive the benefit of the circuit ruling unless the Supreme Court grants a stay or later reverses it.

## Policy & Benefits

### CRUSH would tighten enrollment, overpayment and ownership controls across health programs

The proposal reaches Medicare, Medicare Advantage, Part D, Medicaid, CHIP and the federal Marketplace. CMS estimates nearly $90 billion in ten-year savings, but that is a projected regulatory estimate—not money already recovered. Providers, plans, states, brokers and beneficiary advocates can submit comments through January 19 before CMS decides whether and how to issue a final rule.

[Official CMS overview](https://www.cms.gov/Fraud) · [Official MLN notice](https://www.cms.gov/training-education/medicare-learning-network/newsletter/mln-connects-newsletter-november-17-2026)

### HHS finalizes faster health-record access and broader care-coordination rules

The HIPAA Privacy Rule amendments shorten response time for access requests, clarify access through personal health apps, require advance fee notice, ease identity verification, and expressly permit disclosures to social-service and community organizations for individual care coordination. The rule becomes effective 60 days after Federal Register publication; regulated entities will have a later compliance date specified in the final rule, so the changes are not all operational today.

[Official HHS announcement](https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/hhs-issues-new-final-rule-modernize-hipaa-privacy-rule-support-coordinated-care-individual-engagement-reduce-regulatory-burdens.html) · [Official fact sheet](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/changes-support-coordinated-care-individual-engagement-reduce-regulatory-burdens-final-rule.html)

### DOJ and VA announce actions against 15 defendants in benefits-fraud matters

The nationwide initiative includes newly filed charges and completed cases alleging more than $3.4 million in intended losses involving VA disability payments, survivor payments, a service-disabled-veteran contracting program and exploitation of disabled veterans. Indictments are accusations and the charged defendants are presumed innocent; convictions and sentencings listed in the same announcement are final adjudications unless overturned.

[Official Justice Department announcement](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-va-and-va-oig-announce-enforcement-actions-against-15-defendants)

## Science, Medicine & Public Health

### Sustained measles transmission ends the United States' elimination designation

PAHO's commission uses a 12-month continuity standard, not a simple case-count threshold. Across the Americas, 58,213 confirmed cases and 82 deaths had been reported through November 13, compared with 12,806 cases in the same period of 2025. CDC says restoring U.S. status will require interrupting endemic transmission through vaccination, surveillance and coordinated outbreak response.

[PAHO official announcement](https://www.paho.org/en/news/17-11-2026-paho-urges-countries-step-efforts-stop-measles-outbreaks-and-move-towards) · [CDC joint statement](https://www.cdc.gov/media/releases/2026/loss-of-usmeasles-elimination-status.html)

### FDA approves the first therapy shown to extend survival in infantile-onset NPC

FDA approved intrathecal adrabetadex (Lembaz) for adults and children weighing at least 7 kilograms whose neurological symptoms began before age 6. In 36 treated patients compared with matched external controls, median survival was 8.5 versus 4.9 years and the estimated risk of death was 55% lower. The nonrandomized external-control design limits causal certainty, and the boxed warning covers hearing impairment, including permanent hearing loss.

[Official FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-drug-shown-help-patients-infantile-onset-niemann-pick-disease-type-c-live-longer)

### New Free Journal Articles

### [Physics-informed graph models improve forecasts of tau-PET progression](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13771670/)

Machine Learning: Health published a peer-reviewed human imaging/modeling study using 779 longitudinal tau-PET pairs from 462 amyloid-positive ADNI participants, plus a structural-connectome reference from 84 participants. Adding two biological propagation equations to a graph neural network improved forecasting across the authors' evaluation metrics and produced more localized, biologically plausible attributions. Why it matters: better individualized tau forecasts could aid research and trial design, but this is not a diagnostic test or treatment. Major limitations include a selected ADNI cohort, reliance on model assumptions and internal validation, and no demonstration yet of improved patient outcomes. Published online November 16, 2026.

[Free full text — PubMed Central](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13771670/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/3049-477X/aebb31)

### [A temporary primary-care fee increase did not improve measured SNF outcomes for dual eligibles with dementia](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13776176/)

Health Services Research published a peer-reviewed difference-in-differences study of a national 20% Medicare fee-for-service sample linked to nursing-home assessments from 2012–2019. Using the Affordable Care Act's 2013–2014 Medicaid primary-care fee bump as a natural experiment, researchers found no significant change in visit volume, seven quality measures, skilled-nursing-facility length of stay or Medicare spending for dual-eligible adults age 65 and older with Alzheimer's disease or related dementias. The result is an association from a policy natural experiment—not proof that payment never matters. Limitations include state-level implementation differences, possible unmeasured concurrent changes, fee-for-service-only data and outcomes that may miss care quality. Published online November 17, 2026; the December issue date is not the release date.

[Free full text — PubMed Central](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13776176/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/1475-6773.70181)
