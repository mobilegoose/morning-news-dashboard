## Top 5 Things to Know Today

*Ranked by broad public importance*

### The Senate passes a bipartisan permitting overhaul, 62–33

**1 · CONGRESS · ENERGY & INFRASTRUCTURE**

The bill would shorten federal reviews for energy, transmission and other infrastructure projects, while requiring large data centers to bear specified grid-upgrade costs. It now goes to the House, so it is not yet law. Supporters say the measure could speed construction; environmental groups warn that its limits on review and litigation could weaken safeguards under bedrock environmental statutes.

Sources: [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/b41c2017e4a9fe2e6de92ec77a4cdd0a)

### The Justice Department abandons its effort to delay certification of Alaska’s U.S. Senate election

**2 · ELECTIONS · ALASKA SENATE**

After Mary Peltola won the race, DOJ told the Ninth Circuit that the certification dispute is moot and asked it to vacate the district court’s dismissal. The trial judge had rejected the government’s suit for lack of standing. The filing ends the requested pause; the appellate court still must decide what to do with the lower-court judgment.

Sources: [Alaska Beacon](https://alaskabeacon.com/2026/11/19/doj-abandons-lawsuit-that-sought-to-pause-certification-of-alaskas-us-senate-election/) · [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Politics/doj-dropping-alaska-midterms-senate-certification-lawsuit-case/story?id=138307517)

### The United Kingdom and European Union agree on food rules, carbon markets and youth mobility

**3 · U.K.–EU · POST-BREXIT RESET**

The summit package would create a common sanitary-and-phytosanitary area in March 2028, link carbon markets from 2028 and establish a reciprocal youth-mobility route for up to 100,000 people a year by 2030. Tuition treatment remains unresolved and is due for further negotiation. Several elements still require implementing agreements and legislation.

Sources: [Official U.K. government explainer](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ukeu-summit-2026-key-documents/uk-eu-summit-2026-explainer-html) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/uk/what-eu-uk-have-agreed-summit-2026-11-20/)

### Labor Department issues a new final rule for employee-versus-contractor status

**4 · LABOR · INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS**

The rule rescinds the 2024 framework and makes control and opportunity for profit or loss the two core factors under the Fair Labor Standards Act. Other factors remain relevant when they point in a different direction. It takes effect 60 days after Federal Register publication and does not replace tax, labor-relations or stricter state-law tests.

Sources: [Official DOL rule page](https://www.dol.gov/index.php/agencies/whd/flsa/misclassification/2026rulemaking) · [Official DOL FAQ](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/flsa/misclassification/2026rulemaking/faqs)

### A federal court blocks restrictions on Ryan White HIV funding for providers serving transgender patients

**5 · HEALTHCARE · CIVIL RIGHTS**

The court held that the administration’s funding conditions conflicted with the Ryan White statute and violated constitutional and administrative-law protections. It permanently enjoined enforcement against the provider plaintiffs’ members and vacated the challenged conditions. The ruling protects those providers now; any appeal could test its broader reach.

Sources: [Opinion and order](https://lambdalegal.org/legal_document/aahm_us_20261119_opinion-order/) · [Case explainer](https://lambdalegal.org/newsroom/aahm_us_20261119_federal-court-rules-trump-admin-restrictions-on-ryan-white-funding-for-hiv-care-for-trans-people-unlawful/) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/judge-blocks-trumps-transgender-related-limits-hiv-funding-2026-11-19/)

## Most Useful Development Today

*A practical check under the new worker-classification rule*

### Use DOL’s new contractor-rule FAQ to compare the label with the real working relationship

**WORK CLASSIFICATION · PRACTICAL CHECK**

The practical question is not what the contract calls the worker, but who controls the work and whether managerial skill creates a genuine opportunity for profit or loss. DOL’s FAQ also makes clear that the federal rule does not displace stricter state tests or decide status for taxes or collective-bargaining law. Businesses and workers have a 60-day implementation window after publication.

Sources: [Official DOL FAQ](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/flsa/misclassification/2026rulemaking/faqs) · [Final rule PDF](https://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/WHD/flsa/RIN_1235-AA46.pdf)

## Science, Medicine & Public Health

*Genomics, a rare-disease approval, one free journal article and quantum research*

### A million-person study identifies 58 genomic regions linked to endometriosis

**GENOMICS · ENDOMETRIOSIS**

The NIH-supported analysis included more than one million participants, over 30% with non-European ancestry, and identified 58 associated regions, 27 not previously reported, plus 314 implicated genes and shared genetic links with other conditions. These are population-level associations that sharpen pathway research; they are not a diagnostic test or evidence that any single variant causes disease.

Sources: [NIH release](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/large-nih-supported-study-identifies-key-underlying-pathways-endometriosis) · [Nature Genetics article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-026-02810-9)

### FDA approves the first drug for neurological symptoms of type 3 Gaucher disease

**RECENT FALLBACK · FDA APPROVAL · NOVEMBER 18**

Venglustat (Infilbray) was approved for adults and children weighing at least 15 kilograms. In a randomized, double-blind, multicenter trial of 43 patients, the treated group improved on a combined neurological measure. The small study and rare-disease population limit certainty; the label includes serious psychiatric risks, including suicidal ideation, and the drug is not approved for other Gaucher types.

Sources: [Official FDA announcement](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-drug-treat-neurological-symptoms-type-3-gaucher-disease)

### New Free Journal Articles

### Arginase inhibitor plus chemotherapy shows early response signals in advanced solid tumors

**NEW FREE JOURNAL ARTICLE · CANCER THERAPY · NOVEMBER 17**

Journal of Immunotherapy and Precision Oncology published a peer-reviewed, open-label, nonrandomized phase 1/2 study of 149 adults—54 in phase 1 and 95 in phase 2—with advanced or metastatic solid tumors. INCB001158 increased circulating arginine and, with chemotherapy, produced objective response rates of 24.2% in a 33-person biliary-tract cohort and 16.7% in a 24-person platinum-resistant ovarian-cancer cohort. This is early-stage human evidence, not proof of added benefit or an available treatment: small heterogeneous cohorts, no randomized comparator and combination therapy make the inhibitor’s independent effect uncertain. Published electronically November 17, 2026; free full text.

Sources: [Free full text — PubMed Central](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13777791/) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43054192/) · [Trial record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03314935)

### Google reports a 105-qubit contextuality experiment with error-detection benefits

**QUANTUM COMPUTING · EXPERIMENTAL RESULT**

The experiment achieved 98.3% coordination in a structured task compared with an 88.9% classical benchmark and used contextual correlations to identify some errors. The result is a substantive physics and control demonstration, not general-purpose quantum advantage or a near-term commercial application.
