## 2. Most Useful Development Today

### The challenged voter-list and mail-ballot rules remain blocked in the plaintiff states

In *Trump v. California*, the administration asks the Supreme Court to pause a Massachusetts judge’s injunction while appeals continue. The dispute is whether the president may direct federal agencies to build a citizenship-based voter list and condition postal delivery of ballots, or whether those rules belong to states and Congress.

**Procedural status:** The Court has not granted a stay or decided legality. Responses are due August 3. Unless the justices change the status quo, the injunction remains in force for the 23 plaintiff states and Washington, D.C. [Official application](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/26/26A124/417370/20260727144320600_Trump%20v.%20California%20Application%20and%20Appendix.pdf) · [AP explainer](https://apnews.com/article/trump-mail-voting-appeal-executive-order-supreme-court-5482700c21f4945d6991dff24776b525)

## 4. Supreme Court & Legal

### *Trump v. California*: government asks Court to lift an injunction

California, 22 other states and Washington, D.C., argue that the president exceeded his power by directing USCIS and SSA to create citizenship lists and USPS to restrict mail-ballot delivery. The administration says the order is policy guidance and the challenge came too early. A district judge entered an injunction and the First Circuit declined to stay it; the Solicitor General now asks the Supreme Court for a stay, which would temporarily lift the injunction while litigation proceeds. The Court has only requested responses, due August 3, so no merits ruling or emergency relief has been granted. [Official application and appendix](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/26/26A124/417370/20260727144320600_Trump%20v.%20California%20Application%20and%20Appendix.pdf) · [AP explainer](https://apnews.com/article/trump-mail-voting-appeal-executive-order-supreme-court-5482700c21f4945d6991dff24776b525)
