## Top 5 Things to Know Today

### Russia and Ukraine begin a localized ceasefire around occupied Oleshky

The two sides began a temporary, geographically limited pause so a humanitarian mission can bring aid and evacuate civilians from Oleshky after more than four years of Russian occupation. Moscow continued attacks elsewhere, so this is not a general ceasefire. The immediate test is whether the agreed corridor functions safely and whether civilians can leave without the pause collapsing.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-trump-f26143c32e4673686381d0ff87225720) · [Reuters — potentially limited](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/russia-ukraine-begin-localised-ceasefire-allow-humanitarian-operation-oleshky-2026-10-27/)

### Israel votes in its first national election since October 7

Voting is underway for all 120 Knesset seats, with roughly seven million people eligible and turnout running high in the morning. This is a live election, not a result: polls close at 10 p.m. local time, official counting follows and coalition negotiations may take weeks. The outcome will shape Israel's government after the Gaza war and continuing regional conflict.

[Associated Press live coverage](https://apnews.com/live/israel-election-netanyahu-gaza-mideast-news-updates-10-27-2026) · [AP election guide](https://apnews.com/article/3b38fd7c304ac2a68281a891cbfe6063)

### Egypt authorizes defensive military support for Saudi Arabia

Egypt's parliament voted Monday to let President Abdel-Fattah el-Sissi support Saudi Arabia against Houthi missile and drone attacks. A regional official said the plan involves air-defense systems and aircraft, not ground troops in Yemen; lawmakers received few public details on duration, cost or scope. The vote could widen Egypt's role in a rapidly escalating regional conflict even if Cairo keeps the mission defensive.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/egypt-saudi-arabia-military-support-parliament-houthis-0122d6201f904496a080e60e0f983697) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/26/egyptian-parliament-authorises-military-aid-for-saudi-arabia)

### Federal prosecutors bring a new classified-information case against James Comey

The Justice Department filed a new criminal case accusing former FBI Director James Comey of disclosing classified information connected to a 2016 news story about Yahoo's email scanning. Comey's lawyers say the filing is legally defective and time-barred; those are defenses for the court to resolve, and the charges are allegations, not findings of guilt. A separate case over his congressional testimony was dismissed after the prosecutor's appointment was ruled unlawful.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/8479bdbf1bcdeeae313b453a3cd0c544)

## Most Useful Development Today

### Ask about HRR testing after a new first-line targeted-treatment approval

FDA approved talazoparib with enzalutamide for adults whose metastatic prostate cancer is still sensitive to androgen-pathway treatment and has a qualifying homologous-recombination-repair mutation on an authorized test. In the 599-person randomized TALAPRO-3 trial, radiographic progression-free survival favored the combination (hazard ratio 0.48), while overall-survival data remain immature. This is not for every prostate-cancer patient: ask the oncology team about tumor testing, eligibility, blood-count monitoring and serious risks including myelosuppression and rare MDS/AML.

[FDA approval notice](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-talazoparib-enzalutamide-metastatic-androgen-pathway-modulation-naive-or-sensitive)


## U.S. & World

### Publishers ask India's Supreme Court to block privacy-law provisions

A coalition of 21 publishers opened a Supreme Court challenge arguing that India's new data-protection law could force journalists to obtain consent before publishing personal information and expose confidential sources. The government says the law protects privacy. The Court has not ruled; the case matters because it tests whether privacy safeguards can operate without becoming prior restraint on reporting.

## Supreme Court & Legal

### Ninth Circuit declines DOJ's immediate bid to block about 20,000 ballots

The Justice Department asked an appellate panel to require California to segregate ballots associated with federal registration lists it says may involve noncitizens. The Ninth Circuit declined to intervene before the district judge holds a Tuesday hearing, leaving current ballot handling in place for now. This was an emergency procedural ruling, not a decision on the merits and not proof that anyone on the lists is ineligible.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/ed14390afb8af8fde1c2d0f6a90a0761) · [San Francisco Chronicle](https://www.sfchronicle.com/politics/article/trump-s-california-election-probe-appears-22494907.php)

### Judge rules former prosecutor Maurene Comey's firing unlawful

U.S. District Judge Jesse Furman held that the government violated civil-service protections when it fired former federal prosecutor Maurene Comey and ordered retroactive reinstatement and back pay through December 20, 2025. He did not decide her First Amendment retaliation claim, and DOJ says it will appeal. The ruling rejects the government's broad firing theory but is presently a trial-court judgment focused on her case.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/be7de14f705def3bb6eeadafa7e60805)

### Judge temporarily blocks more tree removal at a federal golf course

U.S. District Judge Ana Reyes barred further tree damage or removal at East Potomac Golf Links and required 10 business days' written notice before irreversible modifications while a preservation group's suit proceeds. A preliminary injunction preserves conditions; it does not finally decide ownership, statutory compliance or the project's legality. The administration says it will appeal.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/f1fafe7da7796e24b2b0626bc9f9ba42)

### Alibaba wins a temporary block on a Pentagon lobbying restriction

A federal judge stopped the government from denying contracts to other companies merely because they or affiliates employ lobbyists who also work for Alibaba. The preliminary injunction does not remove Alibaba from the Pentagon's Chinese-military-company list and does not finally resolve its broader challenge. For now, it narrows how the government may extend consequences to third parties while the case continues.

[Channel NewsAsia](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/business/alibaba-wins-injunction-against-pentagon-contract-restrictions-tied-china-blacklist-6496001) · [Reuters — potentially limited](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/alibaba-wins-injunction-against-pentagon-contract-restrictions-tied-china-blacklist-2026-10-27/)

## Policy & Benefits

### DOJ announces voting-related charges against 48 noncitizens

The Justice Department says cases filed across Pennsylvania, California, Florida, New York and Nevada accuse 48 noncitizens of illegal voting, registration or related offenses. A charging document is an allegation, not a conviction, and the announcement by itself does not establish how common noncitizen voting is. The cases will now proceed individually under different facts and procedural postures.

[Justice Department announcement](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-announces-charges-involving-48-aliens-election-related-crimes)

### DOJ and Education say seven joint guidance documents are not enforceable

The departments published a notice saying they will not treat seven existing civil-rights guidance documents as binding or rely on them when enforcing Titles IV and VI, the Equal Educational Opportunities Act, the ADA or Section 504 while they reassess the documents after Loper Bright. The notice changes the status of guidance, not the underlying statutes or regulations. Schools, students and disabled people should distinguish this enforcement instruction from a repeal of legal rights.

[Official Federal Register public-inspection PDF](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-21685.pdf)


## Science, Medicine & Public Health

### New Free Journal Articles

### Marine compound rapidly kills Naegleria fowleri in laboratory tests

Marine Drugs published a peer-reviewed in-vitro and mechanistic study testing rutamycin B against five Naegleria fowleri strains plus two mammalian cell lines. The compound reached nanomolar potency within four hours, showed a reported selectivity index of 200–300 and acted synergistically with amphotericin B; ATP-synthase inhibition and reactive-oxygen production suggest a possible target. This matters because primary amoebic meningoencephalitis is usually fatal, but it is early laboratory evidence—not animal or human treatment evidence—and blood-brain-barrier penetration, live-model efficacy, dosing and safety remain unknown. Online publication: October 26, 2026.

[Free full text — Marine Drugs](https://www.mdpi.com/1660-3397/24/11/379) · [DOI record](https://doi.org/10.3390/md24110379)

### Florida data analysis maps a concentration of child firearm hospitalizations

KFF Health News analyzed Florida hospital billing data from 2018 through 2024 and found more than 4,000 children hospitalized for firearm injuries statewide, including at least 375 in Jacksonville and over 150 in three ZIP codes that include the former Brown's Dump area. The reporting connects present-day injury patterns with redlining and lead-exposure research. The geographic overlap is important for prevention and investment, but the observational analysis cannot prove that lead or historic housing policy caused any individual shooting.

[KFF Health News data analysis](https://kffhealthnews.org/race-and-health/jacksonville-jax-florida-gun-firearm-violence-redlining-lead-poisoning/)
