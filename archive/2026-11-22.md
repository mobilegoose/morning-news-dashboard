### [Greenwich Rx recalls specified compounded sterile products dispensed nationwide](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/greenwich-rx-issues-voluntary-nationwide-recall-compounded-sterile-products-due-lack-sterility)

PEER-REVIEWED RANDOMIZED-TRIAL SUBGROUP ANALYSIS · ONLINE NOVEMBER 20

### [Nerandomilast in U.S. participants with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00408-026-00965-0)

*Lung.* This prespecified U.S. subgroup of the phase 3 FIBRONEER-IPF randomized placebo-controlled trial included 196 of 1,177 participants across 57 U.S. sites. At week 52, adjusted forced-vital-capacity decline was 257.9 mL with placebo, 152.7 mL with 9 mg nerandomilast (difference 105.2 mL; 95% CI, 13.0–197.4) and 126.2 mL with 18 mg (difference 131.7 mL; 95% CI, 33.8–229.6). Composite clinical-outcome confidence intervals crossed 1, and diarrhea was more common with treatment, especially 18 mg. The result supports lung-function benefit in this subgroup but cannot prove mortality or hospitalization benefit. Important limitations: the subgroup was not powered for clinical events, event counts were low and few Black participants were enrolled. Nerandomilast was already FDA-approved in 2025; this is new subgroup evidence, not a new approval.

[Free full text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00408-026-00965-0)[PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43062734/)

CLAUDE · FORMAL MATHEMATICS

### Anthropic mathematician and Claude claim a proof of the Riemann hypothesis

Levent Alpöge released a four-page note and roughly 17 million lines of Lean code claiming a proof of the Riemann hypothesis and a generalized form for Dirichlet L-functions. Independent groups have reported that the formal archive compiles and that kernel checks pass, which is meaningful evidence that the code proves the stated formal theorems. It is still not an accepted mathematical result: the argument lacks a finished human-readable proof, experts have not reached broad understanding, and the Clay Mathematics Institute still lists the problem as open.

[Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-03812-7)[Independent verification log](https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/~mrubinst/alpoge_claude_grh/)[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/4fe8b69a891f160f9e7f0c85af8c55c8)

### DOJ closes its NextEra–Dominion investigation after a New England capacity-market commitment
