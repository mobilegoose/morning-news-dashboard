**Saturday, November 14, 2026 · Updated after 10:58 a.m. Eastern**

## Top 5 Things to Know Today

### FAA ground stop causes thousands of weekend flight cancellations

A failure in the Federal Aviation Administration's aging air-traffic-control technology triggered a nationwide ground stop Friday lasting more than four hours—the longest such stop in 25 years. More than 2,500 Saturday flights were canceled and hundreds delayed, with international knock-on effects. The FAA said all aircraft already airborne landed safely and no one was endangered, but carriers warned that recovery could take days.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/1fbf4992f0ddaa4c463b0e4990b6e291)

### Supreme Court agrees to review the administration's border-as-invasion asylum ban

In Mullin v. RAICES, the administration asks the Court to reverse lower-court rulings that rejected President Trump's January 2025 proclamation suspending access to asylum and related protections on the theory that unlawful migration constitutes an invasion. The Court granted certiorari Friday on two questions, meaning it will decide the merits—not that it has upheld the policy. The challenged policy remains in force during the litigation; argument is expected this spring, with a decision likely by late June.

[Official Supreme Court docket](https://www.supremecourt.gov/docket/docketfiles/html/public/26-239.html) · [ACLU case explainer](https://www.aclu.org/press-releases/supreme-court-to-hear-challenge-to-trump-proclamation-eliminating-asylum) · [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/aeed6eb39a46b6f9e56f4b6940a5cfe4)

### CMS offers states a six-month transition that can prevent work-rule disenrollments

CMS told states they may use a hold-harmless period through June 30, 2027, as new Medicaid community-engagement requirements begin January 1. Participating states must still assess compliance and exemptions, but may not deny or terminate otherwise eligible people solely for failing the 80-hours-per-month rule during the transition. The option gives states time to test notices and data systems, but it is not an automatic nationwide delay; each state must elect and implement it.

[Official CMS announcement](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-announces-hold-harmless-period-strengthen-medicaid-work-requirement-systems-improve-beneficiary) · [Official State Medicaid Director letter](https://www.medicaid.gov/Federal-Policy-Guidance/Downloads/smd26006.pdf)

### Iran reports three tankers struck mines; Israeli strike kills two in Gaza

Iranian state television said three oil tankers hit naval mines and caught fire near the Strait of Hormuz; it did not identify the vessels' nationalities or report casualties, and independent confirmation was limited at the cutoff. Separately, an Israeli drone strike in Jabaliya killed two Palestinians and wounded several others; Israel said it targeted Hamas militants. Together, the developments add shipping and civilian risk to an already volatile region.

[Associated Press regional update](https://apnews.com/article/ade76bebaf22135168aac6eb3016bc19)

### Second Circuit restores nearly $74 million in highway funds withheld from New York

In State of New York v. Department of Transportation, the Second Circuit set aside the federal government's decision to withhold nearly $74 million in 2027 highway funds over New York's refusal to revoke about 33,000 non-domiciled commercial driver's licenses after holders lost immigration status. The court held that the rules in force did not impose the continuing cancellation duty the agency asserted, making the funding decision arbitrary and contrary to law. The judgment removes that withholding determination; the agency may still act under lawful rules and procedures.

[Official Second Circuit opinion](https://ag.ny.gov/sites/default/files/decisions/new-york-v-united-states-department-of-transportation-order-2026.pdf) · [Justia case page](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca2/26-1097/26-1097-2026-11-13.html) · [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/738429d9600c198cdac285c2d08ccc72)


## Most Useful Development Today

### Medline expands its repair-and-replacement program for recalled beds and rails

Medline updated customers and patients on a recall covering all Medline-brand beds, rails, mattresses and related accessories because of potential fire and entrapment hazards that can cause serious injury or death. Until a bed is repaired or replaced, the FDA-posted instructions say to unplug it except while making a necessary adjustment, never use an extension cord and use a second person for movement when possible. Contact the provider or distributor that supplied the bed to check repair, replacement or refund eligibility; do not attempt an unauthorized repair.

The new air-traffic-control technology overloaded older FAA equipment, creating intermittent communications and radar gaps and forcing the nationwide stop. Airlines then had aircraft and crews out of position, producing cancellations well after the system resumed. Travelers should verify the operating flight—not only the original itinerary—before leaving for the airport and use the carrier's direct rebooking channel.

## Supreme Court & Legal

### What the asylum cert grant means—and what it does not mean

The Refugee and Immigrant Center for Education and Legal Services and other groups sued over a presidential proclamation that treats the southern border situation as an invasion and suspends statutory asylum and related protections. The administration, led on the docket by DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin, asked the Supreme Court to reverse the D.C. Circuit rulings favoring the challengers. Friday's grant limited review to the first two questions in the petition; it sets the case for briefing and argument but does not approve the proclamation. The Court will decide whether the President may suspend these statutory protections and whether courts may review that action, with a merits ruling likely by the end of the term.

[Official docket and filings](https://www.supremecourt.gov/docket/docketfiles/html/public/26-239.html) · [Official November 13 order list](https://www.supremecourt.gov/orders/courtorders/111326zr_09m1.pdf) · [Petition](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/26/26-239/419860/20260824104858426_Mullin_v_RAICES_petition_final.pdf)

### Second Circuit rejects DOT's highway-funding penalty

New York challenged the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration's decision to withhold highway money unless the state canceled thousands of commercial licenses issued to people who later lost lawful status. The court granted New York's petition for review and vacated the agency determination because the cited regulations did not create the continuing revocation obligation the agency claimed. Procedurally, the federal withholding decision is set aside; this is a merits ruling on that agency action, not a blanket right to retain a commercial license regardless of current law.

[Official opinion](https://ag.ny.gov/sites/default/files/decisions/new-york-v-united-states-department-of-transportation-order-2026.pdf) · [Justia docket summary](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca2/26-1097/26-1097-2026-11-13.html)

## Policy & Benefits

### States can postpone adverse action while testing Medicaid work-rule systems

The CMS option matters most at the renewal and notice stages: a participating state continues checking work, volunteer activity and exemptions, but does not close an otherwise eligible case solely for noncompliance before July 1, 2027. Beneficiaries should still respond to state requests and document exemptions; the transition prevents a specified termination ground, not every eligibility closure.

[Official CMS announcement](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-announces-hold-harmless-period-strengthen-medicaid-work-requirement-systems-improve-beneficiary) · [Official guidance](https://www.medicaid.gov/Federal-Policy-Guidance/Downloads/smd26006.pdf)

### Labor Department tells plan fiduciaries to address inaccurate provider directories

The Employee Benefits Security Administration issued an enforcement alert on so-called ghost networks—directories listing clinicians who are unavailable, no longer in network or otherwise inaccurate. The department says employers and other plan fiduciaries remain responsible for prudent monitoring even when a vendor maintains the directory. The alert is guidance and an enforcement signal, not a new regulation; it gives participants a useful basis for documenting failed contacts, unexpected out-of-network costs and complaints.

[Official DOL enforcement alert](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/ebsa/about-ebsa/our-activities/enforcement/enforcement-alert-best-practices-protecting-participants-and-beneficiaries-inaccurate-network)

### FTC and 13 states sue to block a $3.5 billion veterinary-supplies merger

The Federal Trade Commission filed an administrative complaint seeking to stop Covetrus from acquiring rival distributor MWI Veterinary Supply. The agency alleges that combining two leading distributors would reduce competition and raise costs for veterinary clinics; 13 states joined. The 2–0 vote and filed complaint begin litigation—they are not a final finding that the deal is unlawful.

[Official FTC announcement](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/11/ftc-sues-block-veterinary-supplies-distributor-covetruss-35-billion-acquisition-rival-mwi) · [Official case page](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/2610070-cvet-topcomwi-veterinary-supply)


## Science, Medicine & Public Health

### Flu activity reaches unusually high levels early in the season

Seven states reported high influenza activity, with the South and West most affected and Louisiana reporting the highest burden. Federal estimates cited by AP put the season so far at about 2.6 million illnesses, 36,000 hospitalizations and 2,100 deaths; early hospitalization rates are the highest in more than 15 years. Preliminary laboratory evidence suggests this year's vaccine still recognizes the circulating H1N1 variant, but effectiveness estimates are not final.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/615b4eecba876dca82c5dd16fdb29984)

### FDA approves durvalumab plus enfortumab vedotin around surgery for muscle-invasive bladder cancer

FDA approved durvalumab with enfortumab vedotin-ejfv before surgery, followed by durvalumab after radical cystectomy, for adults with muscle-invasive bladder cancer who cannot receive or decline cisplatin. The decision is based on the randomized, open-label VOLGA trial with 695 participants. This is an available regimen for the specified population, not evidence that it is appropriate for every bladder-cancer patient; the FDA page links the prescribing information and safety details.

[Official FDA approval notice](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-durvalumab-enfortumab-vedotin-ejfv-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer)

### New Free Journal Articles

### [Expert review finds sufficient uncertainty to justify a laminoplasty-versus-fusion trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13754368/)

Neurosurgery Practice published a peer-reviewed feasibility study in which 26 cervical-spine experts reviewed 50 trial cases twice, six months apart—2,600 total responses. A majority considered 46 of 50 cases (92%) suitable for randomization between laminoplasty and fusion, supporting the feasibility of a future comparative trial. Why it matters: cervical spondylotic myelopathy can profoundly affect function, yet procedure choice lacks strong randomized evidence. Major limitation: this was expert judgment from imaging and case information, not patient outcomes; it does not show that either operation is safer or more effective. Published online November 12, 2026.

## AI Today

### OpenAI introduces ChatGPT Wallet for approved agent purchases

OpenAI announced a U.S. rollout of ChatGPT Wallet for eligible Pro users age 18 and older. The feature lets an agent complete a purchase only after the user approves it; merchants receive a payment token rather than the underlying card number, and each purchase can require a biometric or other security check. The release adds real transaction capability, so users should review the final merchant, price and dispute protections before approval rather than treating an agent suggestion as authorization.
