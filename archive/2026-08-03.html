1 · Western wildfires

Spokane-area fires destroy about 600 structures and force mass evacuations

Eastern Washington’s fires remained uncontained after destroying homes, businesses and other buildings and prompting evacuation orders affecting roughly 60,000 people, including patients at a VA hospital. No deaths or injuries had been reported in the morning update, but thousands lost power and firefighting aircraft were disrupted by unauthorized drones.

Why it mattersThis is a major, still-active disaster. Evacuation zones, smoke exposure and power conditions can change quickly.

2 · Middle East

New U.S.–Iran talks begin after Trump pauses additional strikes

President Trump said negotiations would begin Monday after Gulf leaders urged restraint. The pause reduces immediate escalation risk but is not a ceasefire or signed settlement; reopening the Strait of Hormuz and Iran’s nuclear program remain central issues.

AP: talks and regional developments

3 · Federal funding

Senate leaders reach a deal designed to avert an election-season shutdown

The bipartisan proposal would fund agencies through December 11. It still requires congressional passage, so it reduces shutdown risk without eliminating it.

AP: funding agreement

4 · Justice Department

Blanche formally terminates the proposed $1.8 billion “anti-weaponization” fund

Acting Attorney General Todd Blanche rescinded the plan to compensate Trump allies after two Republican senators threatened to block his confirmation. DOJ said no money had been distributed and no claims process had begun.

AP: formal rescission

5 · National security

China’s rare-earth controls squeeze a U.S. missile-magnet supply chain

Chinese restrictions are limiting access to specialized rare-earth materials used in high-performance magnets for missiles and other defense systems. U.S. producers are trying to expand domestic capacity, but scaling processing remains slow.

Food safety · active recall

Check eggs for the Midwest Poultry Salmonella recall

FDA says recalled white-shell and brown cage-free eggs were sold under Country Morning, Cal-Maine Sunups, Brookshire’s, Simple Truth and Kroger brands. Affected cartons carry specified plant and date codes and have sell-by or best-by dates from July 20 through August 17.

What to doUse FDA’s exact carton-code table. Do not eat, sell or serve an affected product; discard it or return it.

6. Science, Medicine & Public Health

Cancer treatment development

FDA grants Breakthrough Therapy designation to an investigational pancreatic-cancer drug

Lilly says FDA granted the designation to olomorasib for previously treated advanced pancreatic cancer with a KRAS G12C mutation. The decision is based on preliminary Phase 1/2 evidence and is intended to speed development and review; it is not FDA approval and the drug remains investigational.

Lilly: designation and evidence basis

Wildfire smoke

Smoke exposure is a special concern for chronic conditions

CDC advises people with asthma, heart or lung disease and other chronic conditions to monitor air quality, keep smoke outside and follow existing treatment plans. A well-fitting N95 or P100 can reduce exposure when going outside is unavoidable.
