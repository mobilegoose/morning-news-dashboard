## 1. Top 5 Things to Know Today

### 1. Trump ordered the Pentagon to scale back U.S.–South Korea exercises

President Donald Trump directed the Pentagon to substantially reduce the annual Ulchi Freedom Shield exercises, which began Monday with about 18,000 South Korean troops. He cited cost, Seoul's refusal to support the U.S. war with Iran and his relationship with North Korea's Kim Jong Un; the military has long described the drills as central to alliance readiness.

**Why it matters:** The order changes a major deterrence exercise after North Korea warned of a response and while U.S. carrier forces are shifting away from the western Pacific. ### 2. The Supreme Court denied Trump's request to reconsider the E. Jean Carroll case

In *Trump v. Carroll*, Trump asked the Court to revisit its June refusal to hear his challenge to a $5 million civil judgment. A jury found him liable for sexually abusing Carroll in a department store in the 1990s and defaming her in 2022; Trump disputed the allegations and challenged evidentiary rulings.

The Court **denied rehearing** Monday without explanation. That is not a merits ruling and sets no precedent; it leaves the lower-court judgment intact and closes this petition. A separate challenge involving an $83.3 million defamation judgment remains distinct. [Official order list](https://www.supremecourt.gov/orders/courtorders/081726zor_8m59.pdf) · [Official docket 25-573](https://www.supremecourt.gov/docket/docketfiles/html/public/25-573.html) · [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/769b731baf27199224c30af55cd0a149)

### 3. Indonesia's Flores earthquake death toll rose to 68

**Why it matters:** The province declared a 14-day emergency, and survivors still need food, medicine and clean water after more than 1,500 aftershocks. [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/indonesia-flores-earthquake-landslide-ec45607ccc22d9df2d5cfe4ab82d33eb)

### 4. U.S.-backed Gaza diplomacy moved from Hamas to Netanyahu

U.S. negotiator Jared Kushner held an hourslong meeting with Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu after a rare meeting Sunday with Hamas leader Khalil al-Hayya. The 15-point road map seeks Hamas' disarmament and an eventual Israeli withdrawal; Hamas has accepted it conditionally, while Netanyahu has voiced serious objections.

**Why it matters:** The meetings are a concrete attempt to restart a stalled ceasefire process, but the parties remain divided over sequencing, security and Gaza's governance. [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/kushner-trump-netanyahu-board-of-peace-mladenov-18252d84c29658ca13ac3380743953be)

## 4. Supreme Court & Legal

### *Trump v. Carroll*: rehearing denied, judgment left intact

Trump was the party asking the Supreme Court to act after the Court denied certiorari on June 29. Monday's unexplained **denial of rehearing** means the justices will not reopen that certiorari decision; it does not endorse every part of the lower court's reasoning or decide a new merits question. The $5 million judgment therefore remains in place, and this Supreme Court proceeding is over. ## 6. Science, Medicine & Public Health

### New Free Journal Articles

#### Multiple-system-atrophy subtypes showed similar alpha-synuclein behavior

**Brain Pathology · online August 14, 2026 · peer-reviewed human postmortem and translational laboratory study.** Researchers compared alpha-synuclein aggregates from people with MSA-C and MSA-P using tissue from three brain regions, seeding assays and inoculation into 82 transgenic mice. Across the reported assays, the two clinical subtypes showed similar conformational stability, seeding and mouse disease kinetics, supporting the hypothesis that different clinical patterns may reflect where pathology begins rather than distinct alpha-synuclein “strains.”

**Why it matters:** Multiple system atrophy is on SSA's Compassionate Allowances list; a better mechanism model could guide future biomarker and treatment research. This does **not** establish a diagnostic test or available therapy.

**Material limitations:** The authors say the mouse model does not accurately reproduce human MSA; the work sampled only three brain regions, used small postmortem groups, lacked quantitative regional atrophy/neuron-loss measures and cannot provide high-resolution aggregate structures. [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13476587/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42601815/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/bpa.70134)
