### New Free Journal Articles

**[Merkel cell carcinoma progression in immunosuppressed patients](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13464361/)** — *Acta Dermato-Venereologica*, online August 12, 2026. **Evidence label:** peer-reviewed retrospective human cohort. The center identified 400 patients; 241 had complete follow-up and 81 (33.6%) progressed. Immunosuppression was associated with about twice the risk in unadjusted analysis, but the adjusted overall estimate was uncertain (OR 2.22; p=.06); the adjusted association with soft-tissue metastasis was stronger (OR 3.55; p=.004). This matters because Merkel cell carcinoma is aggressive and on the Compassionate Allowances list. Limitations include single-center retrospective design, incomplete follow-up for 159 patients and subjective clinical variables; association is not causation. [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13464361/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42584920/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.2340/actadv.v106.adv-2026-0443)
