Most Useful Development Today

Air-travel refund and rebooking rights

AIR-TRAVEL RIGHTS · CANCELLATIONS

A canceled flight gives you a refund choice—do not accept a voucher automatically

If the airline cancels your flight and you decline its alternative transportation, federal rules require a prompt refund to the original form of payment, including the ticket price and unused ancillary fees. Check the operating flight before leaving for the airport, save screenshots and receipts, and choose rebooking only if it works better than the refund. The DOT dashboard also shows each carrier's voluntary commitments for meals and hotels during controllable disruptions.

New Free Journal Articles

NEW FREE JOURNAL ARTICLE · RARE SARCOMA · NOVEMBER 13

Small retrospective study identifies a preliminary HER2-targeting signal in desmoplastic small round cell tumors

The Journal of Cancer Research and Clinical Oncology published a peer-reviewed, single-center retrospective study of 11 patients with molecularly confirmed desmoplastic small round cell tumor, a rare aggressive cancer. Five tumors had HER2 IHC 2+ expression but none had HER2 amplification; two heavily pretreated patients received off-label trastuzumab deruxtecan and both initially responded radiologically, with one later progressing and dying and the other maintaining disease control beyond 10 cycles. Why it matters: it supplies a treatment hypothesis for a cancer with few options, not evidence of an established therapy. Major limitations are the tiny, selected cohort, retrospective design, no control group and treatment experience in only two people. Published online November 13, 2026.
