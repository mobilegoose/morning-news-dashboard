### The International Court of Justice holds Myanmar responsible for genocide against the Rohingya

**1 · INTERNATIONAL LAW · ROHINGYA GENOCIDE**

In a 9–6 merits judgment, the ICJ found that Myanmar's forces committed genocide during the 2017 campaign and that the state violated duties to prevent and punish genocide and enact required domestic law. The Court ordered safe return conditions, reparations and a compliance report within two years; a later phase will determine compensation. The judgment is final and binding, although the Court has no independent enforcement arm.

### The Ninth Circuit temporarily permits enforcement of Venezuela TPS terminations while a Supreme Court request remains pending

**3 · IMMIGRATION · VENEZUELAN TPS**

TPS holders and the National TPS Alliance say DHS unlawfully ended protections for Venezuelans; the government is asking the Supreme Court to pause a district-court judgment restoring them. ### A federal judge finds an ICE official and the government in contempt over immediate re-arrests

**4 · FEDERAL COURTS · ICE CONTEMPT**

Judge Charlotte Sweeney found that ICE made a mockery of release orders by immediately re-arresting at least 18 people outside Colorado's detention facility between October 27 and November 4. This is a civil contempt finding, not a criminal conviction; the sworn policy change is enforceable and future violations could bring sanctions.

## Science, Medicine & Public Health

*An FDA cancer approval and one new free Long COVID review*

### FDA grants accelerated approval to neladalkib for previously treated ALK-positive lung cancer

**RECENT FALLBACK · FDA ACCELERATED APPROVAL · NOVEMBER 17**

FDA authorized neladalkib for adults with locally advanced or metastatic ALK-positive non-small cell lung cancer after specified prior ALK-targeted therapy. In two single-arm ALKOVE-1 cohorts of 67 and 155 patients, confirmed response rates were 49% and 32%; about half of responders in each cohort maintained a response for at least 12 months. The approval is based on response rather than proven survival benefit, so continued authorization depends on confirmatory evidence. Warnings include liver, kidney, eye, muscle and fetal risks.

Sources: [Official FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-neladalkib-previously-treated-locally-advanced-or-metastatic-alk)

### New Free Journal Articles

### Echoes of infection: viral persistence, immune activation, and histopathological changes in long COVID: a systematic review

**NEW FREE JOURNAL ARTICLE · LONG COVID · NOVEMBER 17**

Clinical and Experimental Medicine published a peer-reviewed PRISMA systematic review of 45 human reports: 16 symptom-defined Long COVID studies at least 12 weeks after infection, 23 mixed or post-acute studies, three autopsy reports and three isolated case or fetal reports. The literature found viral RNA, proteins or related markers in selected blood and tissues alongside inflammatory, fibrotic and vascular changes, but results varied widely by population, timing, specimen and assay; no included study recovered replication-competent virus. Why it matters: the review organizes biological evidence relevant to mechanism and biomarker research, but it does not establish that persistence causes every case or that an antiviral treatment works. Major limitations include overlapping cohorts, repeated sampling, inconsistent definitions, biopsy-selection bias, acute-severity and comorbidity confounding, and incomplete controls, so no single participant total is reliable. Published online November 17, 2026; open access under CC BY.

Sources: [Free full text — Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10238-026-02339-5) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43050005/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10238-026-02339-5)
