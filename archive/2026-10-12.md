## Top 5 Things to Know Today

### Federal agents search Rep. Ilhan Omar’s home

Homeland Security Investigations agents executed a judicially authorized warrant at the Minnesota Democrat’s Washington-area home as part of an ongoing criminal investigation. The Justice Department disclosed no allegation, charge or evidence, and Omar had not commented by the cutoff. The search of a sitting member of Congress is a major law-enforcement action, but a warrant is not a finding of guilt.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/8e18c2aea948bd90f4d7a4eb32b9ec3a)

### Russian strikes undercut Trump’s unconfirmed energy-ceasefire claim

Ukraine said Russian attacks hit energy infrastructure in Kharkiv and elsewhere after President Trump announced an energy ceasefire that neither Moscow nor Kyiv had confirmed. Kyiv said it would stop striking Russian refineries if Russia reciprocates. Separately, the European Union imposed its largest single package of individual listings of the war, targeting nearly 1,600 people and entities linked to Russian drone, missile and arms production.

[Associated Press: strikes and ceasefire claim](https://apnews.com/article/6fdef6ff4d033b379c74e1d62183d078) · [Associated Press: EU sanctions](https://apnews.com/article/12e244a70e6930f47acefde1754fa264)

### Saudi Arabia investigates the air-defense failure behind the Riyadh airport attack

Saudi Arabia opened an inquiry into why its air defenses failed to stop the Houthi missile-and-drone barrage that killed at least 13 people and injured more than 300 at King Khalid International Airport. Officials said drones may have been used to saturate defenses before cruise and ballistic missiles arrived. The investigation matters because the attack exposed a major vulnerability in a widening regional conflict.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/40c6ab822df376688ce1a0955a044488)

### Federal judge stops taxpayer-funded promotional ads featuring Trump

U.S. District Judge Jennifer Rochon issued a two-week temporary restraining order requiring the administration to cancel the remainder of a $20 million television campaign funded through DHS. The challengers argue that the spots are unauthorized political propaganda; the judge found they were likely to succeed and faced irreparable harm. The order is temporary and does not finally resolve the lawsuit.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/f845554063cea0b4b41275d32abd3633)

### Robert Hall and Michael Woodford win for work on managing economic shocks

The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences awarded the 2026 economics prize to the two U.S. economists for models that connect household and business decisions to inflation, employment and stabilization policy. Their work shaped modern central-bank tools, including the management of expectations and forward guidance, and is especially relevant during energy-price shocks.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/96f32f1aaeff1054e2dd653e6ea86268) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/hall-woodford-win-2026-nobel-economics-prize-2026-10-12/)

## Supreme Court & Legal

### What is known—and not known—about the Omar search

DOJ confirmed only that Homeland Security Investigations used a judicially authorized warrant in an ongoing criminal investigation. Officials did not disclose the suspected offense, the warrant materials, what was seized or whether Omar is a target; no charge has been announced. Those gaps are legally important because a probable-cause warrant authorizes a search, not a conviction or even necessarily a charge against the resident.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/8e18c2aea948bd90f4d7a4eb32b9ec3a)

### Judge temporarily halts tree removal at East Potomac Golf Links

U.S. District Judge Ana Reyes stopped additional cutting after preservation groups said more than 500 trees had already been removed and argued that the National Park Service avoided required environmental and historic-preservation reviews. The government calls the work maintenance. The short-term order preserves the site while the court considers a preliminary injunction; it does not decide the project’s legality.

## Science, Medicine & Public Health

### Airway microbiota patterns track with Long COVID severity and symptom profiles

European Respiratory Journal · online October 11, 2026 · prospective multicenter PHOSP-COVID cohort of 771 adults previously hospitalized with COVID-19. Oral and sputum microbial profiles at six months differed across pulmonary, neurocognitive and gastrointestinal symptom groups, and some lactic-acid bacteria correlated with inflammatory proteins. These are associations, not proof that the microbes cause symptoms or that changing the microbiome would treat Long COVID; the cohort also does not represent people whose acute infection was never hospitalized.

[Study abstract and metadata](https://www.lifescience.net/publications/2247889/sputum-and-oral-microbiota-profiles-are-associated/) · [European Respiratory Society summary](https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-10-severe-covid-linked-differences-lung.html) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1183/13993003.01162-2026)

### Updated guideline emphasizes CBT, hearing evaluation and self-harm screening

The American Academy of Otolaryngology–Head and Neck Surgery guideline for adults with persistent, bothersome tinnitus recommends targeted history and examination, comprehensive audiology, education, CBT and hearing-device evaluation when appropriate. It recommends against routine imaging without warning signs and against supplements, routine drug treatment solely for tinnitus and repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation. The guidance was released October 9 and newly indexed today; it excludes many secondary causes and is not a substitute for individual evaluation, especially for pulsatile, one-sided or neurologically accompanied symptoms.

[AAO-HNS guideline resource](https://www.entnet.org/resource/cpg-tinnitus-update/) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42861486/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/ohn.70418)

### New Free Journal Articles

#### [Low-input MYD88/CD79B assay improves detection in rare eye and brain lymphomas](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13653407/)

Experimental Hematology & Oncology · online October 9, 2026 · paired laboratory validation in 46 vitreous-fluid or cerebrospinal-fluid specimens. The multiplex PCR/Sanger workflow reported 81.8% sensitivity, 100% specificity and 94.7% accuracy while using much less DNA than the existing MYD88-only test and adding CD79B variants. Why it matters: fragile, cell-poor specimens make primary vitreoretinal and central-nervous-system lymphoma difficult to diagnose. Important limits: this was a small specimen study, not a treatment trial; estimates need broader external validation and a negative result cannot rule out disease.

[Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13653407/) · [Official journal page](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40164-026-00836-7) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42855719/)
