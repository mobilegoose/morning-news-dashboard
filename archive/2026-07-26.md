## 2. Most Useful Development Today

### The ADA turns 36: check which civil-rights protections apply

The ADA bars disability discrimination in employment, state and local services, transit, public accommodations and telecommunications. Start with DOJ’s coverage guide; if the facts appear to involve a violation, use its complaint-routing page and preserve dates, requests, responses and evidence of barriers. [DOJ ADA guide](https://www.ada.gov/topics/intro-to-ada/) · [DOJ complaint guide](https://www.ada.gov/file-a-complaint/) · [Census anniversary facts](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/facts-for-features/2026/disabilities-act.html)
