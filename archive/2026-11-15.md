## Most Useful Development Today

### A canceled flight gives you a refund choice—do not accept a voucher automatically

If the airline cancels your flight and you decline its alternative transportation, federal rules require a prompt refund to the original form of payment, including the ticket price and unused ancillary fees. Check the operating flight before leaving for the airport, save screenshots and receipts, and choose rebooking only if it works better than the refund. The DOT dashboard also shows each carrier's voluntary commitments for meals and hotels during controllable disruptions.

### CDC renews the Ebola-related entry suspension for another 30 days

CDC extended through December 10 its temporary order covering certain non-U.S. citizens who were in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda or South Sudan during the prior 21 days, citing the Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak and cross-border spread. ## Science, Medicine & Public Health

### New Free Journal Articles

### [Small retrospective study identifies a preliminary HER2-targeting signal in desmoplastic small round cell tumors](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13768494/)

The Journal of Cancer Research and Clinical Oncology published a peer-reviewed, single-center retrospective study of 11 patients with molecularly confirmed desmoplastic small round cell tumor, a rare aggressive cancer. Five tumors had HER2 IHC 2+ expression but none had HER2 amplification; two heavily pretreated patients received off-label trastuzumab deruxtecan and both initially responded radiologically, with one later progressing and dying and the other maintaining disease control beyond 10 cycles. Why it matters: it supplies a treatment hypothesis for a cancer with few options, not evidence of an established therapy. Major limitations are the tiny, selected cohort, retrospective design, no control group and treatment experience in only two people. Published online November 13, 2026.

## AI Today

### GitHub adds REST controls for repository-wide AI security scans

GitHub's public preview now lets organization owners, security managers and repository administrators enable or disable AI Scan for repositories through REST endpoints, making scripted or infrastructure-as-code rollout possible. Enabling the setting does not launch a scan; users with write access start scans on demand, and each run consumes AI credits. GitHub Enterprise Server is not supported.

[Official GitHub changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-13-manage-ai-scan-for-repositories-using-the-rest-api/)

### Anthropic launches a self-hosted version of Claude Managed Agents

Organizations accepted into the release can run the Managed Agents server, database and sandboxes on their own infrastructure and keep agent, session and history data there. The service can send model requests to a provider the customer chooses, with Anthropic as the default. This is an access-gated deployment option, not a new general-purpose model, and customers assume operational and security responsibility for the self-hosted stack.
