## Most Useful Development Today

### [Lawfully present SSI recipients challenge planned Medicaid cutoff](https://www.newsfromthestates.com/article/2-refugees-who-settled-providence-join-nationwide-legal-challenge-medicaid-cuts-immigrants)

*MEDICAID · SSI · LAWSUIT FILED*

Five refugees and other humanitarian immigrants who receive SSI filed Mahmoud v. Oz in Rhode Island federal court and seek a nationwide class. ### [Khaya v. Cook County will proceed to argument despite a mootness request](https://www.supremecourt.gov/orders/courtorders/112426zr_q86b.pdf)

*SUPREME COURT · LATE-YESTERDAY ORDER AND FINAL CHECK*

Christopher Khaya and advocacy groups are challenging Cook County's ban on AR-15-platform and similar semiautomatic rifles, arguing that the Second and Fourteenth Amendments protect possession of those arms. After the original lead petitioner left the case, Cook County asked the Supreme Court to dismiss it as moot. The Court's November 24 order deferred that request until oral argument, meaning the Justices will hear the jurisdictional issue along with the dispute on December 2 rather than ending the case now. The order does not decide mootness or the constitutionality of the ban. A final November 25 check found no newer merits opinion, emergency order, cert grant or important action.

## New Free Journal Articles

### [Prehospital antibiotics for patients with suspected sepsis: the PITSTOP-Antibiotics trial](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2857281)

*PEER-REVIEWED RANDOMIZED CLINICAL TRIAL · ONLINE NOVEMBER 24*

JAMA. In a multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled 2×2 factorial trial involving four Ontario paramedic services, 2,048 patients were analyzed (1,026 ceftriaxone; 1,022 placebo; median age 75). Thirty-day mortality was 18.0% versus 18.4% (risk ratio 0.97; 95% CI, 0.81–1.17; P=.77), and all 17 secondary outcomes were similar. This does not support routine prehospital ceftriaxone before emergency-department care for this population; it does not show that timely antibiotics are unimportant in confirmed sepsis. Limitations include a broad suspected-sepsis definition, no pre-antibiotic blood cultures, missing pre-enrollment trajectory and limited power for small effects.

[Free full text](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2857281) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43076168/)

### [Noninvasive breathomics for developing carbonyl biomarkers in esophageal cancer](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44276-026-00253-7)

*PEER-REVIEWED OBSERVATIONAL DIAGNOSTIC STUDY · ONLINE NOVEMBER 23*

BJC Reports. Researchers compared exhaled-breath condensate from 106 people with esophageal cancer and 47 healthy controls after exclusions, with smaller plasma and saliva subsets. Breath acetaldehyde and formaldehyde showed AUCs of 0.77 and 0.87, respectively, but this is early biomarker-development evidence—not an available screening test. Limitations include the single-center design, healthy rather than clinically similar controls, no external validation, and unresolved biological and genetic confounding.

[Free full text](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44276-026-00253-7) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43073384/)
