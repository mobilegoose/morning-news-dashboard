Compassionate Allowances · Clinical pathway study

Dravet syndrome diagnosis often followed a long post-seizure delay

Epilepsia Open · online August 13, 2026. In a peer-reviewed survey of 53 European physicians with 45 reconstructed patient pathways, median diagnosis age was 15 months and the median first-seizure-to-diagnosis delay was 11 months; one quarter were diagnosed after 23 months. Genetic-test access and late expert-center referral were recurring bottlenecks.

Why it matters Earlier recognition can reduce exposure to unsuitable medication in a severe epilepsy covered by SSA’s Compassionate Allowances. Limitations: small specialist-selected, clinician-reported sample; it cannot establish treatment outcomes or represent every health system.

Cancer · Retrospective cohort

Immune changes were associated with prognosis in advanced liver cancer

Clinical & Translational Immunology · online August 14, 2026. In 67 people with HBV-related unresectable hepatocellular carcinoma treated with TACE, immunotherapy and anti-VEGF/TKIs, later-treatment immune measures were associated with survival; an internally derived score separated higher- and lower-risk groups.

Why it matters The markers may support prognostic research, but this is association—not proof that changing the markers improves survival. Limitations: small single-center retrospective cohort, heterogeneous regimens and no external validation.

Dementia · Diagnostic machine learning

Speech measures differentiated two primary progressive aphasia subtypes

Alzheimer’s & Dementia: Diagnosis, Assessment & Disease Monitoring · online August 14, 2026. A peer-reviewed connected-speech study included 84 Italian-speaking patients—23 nonfluent, 23 semantic and 38 logopenic PPA. A model distinguished logopenic from nonfluent PPA with 81.7% accuracy; three-way accuracy was 63.9%.

Why it matters Automated speech analysis could eventually support more accessible assessment. Limitations: small language-specific sample and no external prospective validation; it is not a replacement for specialist diagnosis.
