New Free Journal Articles · Prospective human cohort

Glucose metabolism across COVID-19 and Long COVID

Journal of the Endocrine Society · online August 12, 2026. This peer-reviewed longitudinal RECOVER study followed 821 adults who were enrolled before their first study-documented infection and had pre- and post-infection visits; people with preexisting diabetes or pregnancy were excluded. Mean HbA1c was essentially unchanged through 12 months (5.39% to 5.40%; p=.435), and trends were similar across Long COVID groups. Four participants developed diabetes; 81 met prediabetes criteria during follow-up, but the study did not establish that infection caused those outcomes.

Why it matters Pre-infection measurements reduce an important source of bias and do not support a broad one-year deterioration in average glycemia in this cohort. Limitations: this was observational without a concurrently analyzed uninfected control group; not every participant had every biomarker, and Long COVID grouping relied on self-reported symptoms and maximum symptom scores.
