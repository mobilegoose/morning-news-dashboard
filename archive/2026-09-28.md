# Morning News Dashboard — September 28, 2026

*Updated after 10:30 a.m. Eastern. Fallbacks are labeled with their release date.*

## Top 5 Things to Know Today

1. **Washington and Beijing agree to tariff cuts covering $60 billion in trade.** The governments released reciprocal lists covering about $30 billion of non-sensitive goods each and extended their trade truce through January 10. This is narrower than a broad trade settlement but concretely reduces barriers. [The Business Times / Reuters](https://www.businesstimes.com.sg/international/china-us-agree-tariff-cuts-us60-billion-goods-including-agriculture-household-items)
2. **Qatar begins separate U.S.–Iran meetings around a revised ceasefire proposal.** The plan links Iran-and-Lebanon ceasefires, sanctions and blocked assets, reopening the Strait of Hormuz and renewed nuclear talks. Separate meetings are mediation, not a ceasefire. [Reuters — potentially limited](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-iran-set-hold-separate-talks-with-mediators-monday-or-tuesday-official-says-2026-09-28/)
3. **Transportation Department finalizes weaker fuel-economy standards.** The rule replaces the prior trajectory with a roughly 34.5-mpg fleetwide target for 2031; the administration predicts lower vehicle costs, while fuel use and pollution would rise and litigation is likely. [U.S. DOT](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/president-trump-transportation-secretary-duffy-finalize-freedom-means-affordable-cars) · [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/trump-gas-mileage-electric-vehicles-pollution-403742074c3f3140aaa1d10829daf35f)
4. **Starship reaches orbit for the first time.** Flight 14 deployed Starship’s first Starlink payloads, although one engine failed. [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/spacex-starship-orbit-262d3c58d56bf7a525b49115d6c5dfe8) · [SpaceX](https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-14)
5. **FTC and states settle pesticide antitrust claims against Corteva.** The proposed settlement ends an alleged generic-blocking loyalty program, imposes a 10-year restriction and includes a $35 million state payment; court approval is required and the Syngenta case continues. [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/09/ftc-states-win-protections-lower-pesticide-prices-american-farmers-antitrust-case-against-corteva)

### Recent fallback — September 23: pediatric disability dental access

HHS awarded $51 million to 28 HRSA-supported health centers to build dental services tailored to children with autism, cerebral palsy, Down syndrome and other neurodevelopmental conditions. **Practical step:** use the award announcement to identify funded centers and ask when services begin; the award does not mean every center is immediately offering them.
