### New Free Journal Articles

**[Glucose metabolism across COVID-19 and Long COVID](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13462795/)** — *Journal of the Endocrine Society*, online August 12, 2026. **Evidence label:** peer-reviewed prospective human cohort. RECOVER followed 821 adults enrolled before their first study-documented infection with pre- and post-infection visits; preexisting diabetes and pregnancy were excluded. Mean HbA1c was unchanged through 12 months (5.39% to 5.40%; p=.435), with similar trends across Long COVID groups. Four participants developed diabetes and 81 met prediabetes criteria, but the observational study did not establish infection as the cause. The pre-infection measurements reduce baseline confounding and do not support a broad one-year deterioration in average glycemia in this cohort. Limitations include no concurrently analyzed uninfected control group, incomplete biomarker data and self-reported Long COVID symptoms summarized by maximum score. [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13462795/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42591780/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1210/jendso/bvag173)
