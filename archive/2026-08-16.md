## 6. Science, Medicine & Public Health

### New Free Journal Articles

**[From first seizure to specific antiseizure medication in Dravet syndrome](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13473756/)** — *Epilepsia Open*, online August 13, 2026. **Evidence label:** peer-reviewed European clinician survey with 45 reconstructed patient pathways. Fifty-three physicians reported a median diagnosis age of 15 months and a median 11-month delay from first seizure to diagnosis; one quarter were diagnosed after 23 months. Access to genetic testing and late expert-center referral were recurring bottlenecks. This matters for a Compassionate Allowances condition where earlier recognition can reduce exposure to unsuitable medication, but the small specialist-selected, clinician-reported sample cannot establish treatment outcomes or represent every health system. [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13473756/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42596591/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/epi4.70328)

**[Peripheral immune dynamics during treatment predict prognosis in hepatocellular carcinoma](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13476161/)** — *Clinical & Translational Immunology*, online August 14, 2026. **Evidence label:** peer-reviewed single-center retrospective cohort. In 67 people with HBV-related unresectable liver cancer treated with TACE plus immunotherapy and anti-VEGF/TKIs, later-treatment immune measures were associated with survival; an internally derived score separated higher- and lower-risk groups. This is potentially useful prognostic research, not proof that changing those markers improves survival. The small, retrospective, single-center cohort, heterogeneous regimens and lack of external validation materially limit clinical use. [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13476161/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42603880/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/cti2.70115)

**[Automated differentiation of non-fluent and logopenic primary progressive aphasia using speech measures](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13474157/)** — *Alzheimer’s & Dementia: Diagnosis, Assessment & Disease Monitoring*, online August 14, 2026. **Evidence label:** peer-reviewed diagnostic machine-learning study. A connected-speech model trained on 84 Italian-speaking patients—23 nonfluent, 23 semantic and 38 logopenic PPA—distinguished logopenic from nonfluent PPA with 81.7% accuracy; three-way accuracy was 63.9%. The work could inform accessible diagnostic support, but the small language-specific sample and absence of external prospective validation mean it is not a clinical replacement for specialist assessment. [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13474157/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42602553/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/dad2.70450)
