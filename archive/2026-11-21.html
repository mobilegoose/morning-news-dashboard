Ranked by broad public importance

1 · NATO · POLAND

Russian missiles land in Poland during a major attack on Ukraine

Two Russian missiles and a suspected drone came down in Poland on Friday while European troops were conducting exercises. Poland's prime minister called the episode likely intentional; NATO called it a grave airspace violation and said allied jets had supported Poland's response. No casualty report accompanied the initial account, but the incident raises the risk of direct NATO–Russia escalation.

Associated Press

2 · AUTONOMOUS WEAPONS · UNITED NATIONS

States agree to two more years of talks on lethal autonomous weapons—but no binding rules

The 128 parties to the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons ended overnight negotiations with a mandate to develop possible rules by 2028. Roughly 100 countries supported binding limits, but opposition from major military powers left the final text noncommittal. The agreement keeps diplomacy alive while allowing deployment to outpace regulation.

ABC News / Associated PressReuters

3 · EXECUTIVE POWER · CONGRESSIONAL OVERSIGHT

A new executive order tells federal contractors to route congressional information requests through the White House

Executive Order 14438 requires contractors and subcontractors to notify the White House and contracting agencies about congressional requests involving executive-branch work, follow instructions to withhold information deemed privileged, and face possible contract termination, suspension or debarment for noncompliance. The order immediately changes how Congress may obtain information from private companies doing federal work and is likely to prompt separation-of-powers disputes.

Official executive order

4 · MEDICARE ADVANTAGE · PROPOSED RULE

CMS proposes broad 2028 changes to Medicare Advantage and Part D

The proposal would add 90-day continuity of care after significant provider terminations, cap certain emergency and ambulance cost sharing, revise special-needs-plan coordination, require API-based prior authorization transactions, change broker compensation and overhaul quality ratings in selected states. It is a proposal, not current coverage law; comments are due 60 days after Federal Register publication.

CMS fact sheetFederal Register document

5 · PUBLIC HEALTH · MEASLES

CDC raises the 2026 measles count to 5,838 confirmed cases

The November 20 update reports 43 outbreaks, with 96% of confirmed cases linked to outbreaks and six deaths in CDC's preliminary national accounting. Kindergarten MMR coverage is 92.4%, below the 95% community-protection target. The update is especially important before holiday travel because measles spreads through the air and the United States lost elimination status this year.

UNITED NATIONS · AI WEAPONS

Geneva talks preserve a path toward autonomous-weapons rules without committing to a treaty

The parties agreed to continue drafting possible controls through 2028 after Russia and other major weapons states resisted a binding timetable. The result is procedurally concrete but substantively weak: future negotiations may still produce a protocol, yet the current agreement itself does not prohibit or regulate any weapon.

FEDERAL CONTRACTORS · CONGRESS

Executive Order 14438 creates a White House-controlled process for responding to Congress

Contractors must report pending and future requests about their executive-branch work and comply with instructions about privilege. Agencies must incorporate the requirements into contracts and may consider termination or debarment. The order asserts executive privilege over some contractor communications; it does not itself resolve whether courts will accept that assertion against a valid congressional demand.

Supreme Court & Legal

Two consequential lower-court rulings and the final Court check

FIFTH CIRCUIT · PREGNANT WORKERS FAIRNESS ACT

En banc Fifth Circuit holds pandemic-era House proxy voting unconstitutional, 9–8

In State of Texas v. Blanche, Texas argued that the 2022 appropriations act—including the Pregnant Workers Fairness Act—was invalid because the House counted remotely cast proxies toward a quorum. The court affirmed an injunction preventing federal enforcement of that workplace law against Texas only. The narrow remedy leaves the statute in force elsewhere, but the reasoning invites challenges to other provisions enacted through proxy voting.

HUD · HOMELESSNESS FUNDING

A federal judge invalidates key parts of HUD's 2026 Continuum of Care overhaul

The court found that HUD exceeded the McKinney-Vento Act by using added criteria to bypass Congress's mandatory factors, including performance and movement into permanent housing. It also rejected restrictions involving harm-reduction services and self-sufficiency. HUD must allow amended applications by December 1 and make awards by December 31; the agency may appeal.

MEDICARE ADVANTAGE · PART D

CMS opens a 60-day comment period on major 2028 program changes

Beyond continuity-of-care and cost-sharing protections, CMS proposes a mandatory ten-year model in selected states, a localized replacement for Star Ratings, targeted supplemental benefits for some low-income beneficiaries, and new requirements for prior-authorization data exchange. The proposal also would remove federal caps on broker compensation while adding outlier review—an important consumer-protection tradeoff to scrutinize.

ORIGINAL MEDICARE · CHRONIC DISEASE

HHS awards $47 million to 15 organizations for Medicare prevention demonstrations

The first MAHA ELEVATE cohort will test nutrition, activity, occupational-therapy, fall-prevention and remote-monitoring interventions for conditions including heart failure, dementia, diabetes, obesity and hypertension. These are three-year evidence-building demonstrations for Original Medicare, not new nationwide covered benefits. CMS says outcomes and costs will inform possible later coverage or models.

CDC's preliminary count through November 19 covers 47 jurisdictions and 43 outbreaks; 5,629 cases are outbreak-associated. The agency reports six deaths and kindergarten MMR coverage of 92.4%. Counts can change as state investigations are reconciled, but the update documents continued sustained transmission rather than a forecast.

FDA SAFETY ALERT · VASCULAR GUIDEWIRES

FDA warns clinicians and facilities to stop using specified Heraeus vascular guidewires

Affected Navilyst, Spectrum and Arrow lots may develop clumps in their hydrophilic coating that can detach into the bloodstream and cause thromboembolic injury. FDA says users should immediately stop distribution, quarantine the listed lots and notify downstream customers. No serious injuries or deaths had been reported as of November 5, but the agency classifies the issue as potentially high risk.

AI FOR SCIENCE · BENCHMARK

OpenAI releases MedChemBench for medicinal-chemistry reasoning

The benchmark contains 99 retrospective problems drawn from 117 papers and patents and tests molecular reconstruction, evidence-based explanation, compound selection and property prediction. GPT-6.2 Astra earned 84.5% at xhigh, but selection and numerical prediction remained much weaker than structure reconstruction. OpenAI expressly cautions that the benchmark does not show that a model can discover drugs or improve laboratory decisions.
