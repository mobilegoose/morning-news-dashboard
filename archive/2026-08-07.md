# Morning News Dashboard

**Friday, August 7, 2026**  
Updated after the final same-morning Supreme Court check.

## 1. Top 5 Things to Know Today

### 1. U.S. payrolls fell by 23,000 in July; unemployment held at 4.1%

The Bureau of Labor Statistics said both measures changed little statistically, but revisions also cut May and June payrolls by a combined 103,000. Labor-force participation fell to 61.4%, the lowest in nearly five and a half years. **Why it matters:** the report signals a softer labor market even though one month’s estimate is not a recession verdict. [BLS full report](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/pdf/empsit.pdf) · [Readable Guardian coverage](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/aug/07/july-jobs-report-middle-east-conflict)

### 2. Trump signed two immigration orders on birth tourism and citizenship

One order directs State and Homeland Security to develop rules or guidance to deny entry when the primary purpose of a nonimmigrant visit is giving birth. The second revives limits on birthright citizenship after the Supreme Court’s June merits ruling rejected the administration’s earlier restrictions. Neither order erases citizenship immediately; implementation and litigation will determine practical reach. [Birth-tourism order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/08/ending-birth-tourism/) · [Citizenship order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/08/continuing-to-protect-the-meaning-and-value-of-american-citizenship/) · [AP explainer](https://apnews.com/article/trump-border-immigration-birthright-citizenship-494add8239eb1c0c9f4ccb45db03f1f0)

### 3. FCC voted 2–1 to end the 39% national television-ownership cap

The agency replaced the cap with deal-by-deal public-interest review. The majority says this updates an obsolete rule; the dissent says Congress set the limit and the FCC lacks authority to remove it. **Why it matters:** the vote could enable larger broadcast combinations, but court challenges are likely. [FCC order summary](https://www.fcc.gov/document/fcc-replaces-national-broadcast-ownership-cap) · [Readable Axios coverage](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/06/fcc-broadcast-ownership-cap-vote)

### 4. Saudi Arabia, Turkey and Pakistan signed a mutual-defense pact

The Mecca agreement treats an attack on one country as an attack on all three. It links three Sunni-majority U.S. partners as Saudi Arabia warns of possible attacks from Iranian-aligned forces. **Why it matters:** the pact materially changes regional security commitments during the Iran war. [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/58048d4a100befd4d2c18e0cbae58b7c)

### 5. FDA accelerated approval of a viral immunotherapy for treatment-resistant advanced melanoma

FDA approved Tudriqev with nivolumab for adults with unresectable advanced cutaneous melanoma that progressed after at least eight weeks of anti-PD-1 treatment. In the single-arm trial, 24% of 91 evaluable patients responded; continued approval depends on confirmatory evidence. **Why it matters:** it adds an option for a hard-to-treat group, but the response-rate endpoint and nonrandomized design limit certainty. [FDA approval, evidence and safety](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-new-engineered-viral-immunotherapy-patients-treatment-resistant-advanced-melanoma)

## 2. Most Useful Development Today

### Read the jobs report as a weakening signal, not a stand-alone diagnosis

The headline payroll estimate was negative, but BLS characterized employment and unemployment as little changed. The more useful signal is the combination of the July estimate, 103,000 in downward revisions, and lower participation. Workers and claimants should not infer an individual eligibility decision from national figures; policymakers and households should watch the next report and weekly claims for confirmation. [BLS full report](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/pdf/empsit.pdf)
