## 2. Most Useful Development Today

### Check recalled shredded iceberg lettuce

CDC says implicated Taylor Farms products were distributed in 27 states. Use the product names, codes and distribution list to identify affected lettuce, then discard or return it. Cyclospora commonly causes prolonged watery diarrhea; contact a clinician for persistent or severe symptoms. [CDC recall details](https://tools.cdc.gov/podcasts/download.asp?c=766042&m=316422) · [CDC timeline](https://www.cdc.gov/cyclosporiasis/outbreaks/07-26/timeline.html)
