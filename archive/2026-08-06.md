## 1. Top 5 Things to Know Today

### 1. Iran and Oman reached an understanding on a Hormuz shipping route, but not a final security deal

Iran’s Foreign Ministry said the two sides agreed on the route’s coordinates and were preparing a joint announcement. Consequential operating and security terms remain unsettled, so this is progress rather than a reopened, guaranteed-safe strait. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-oman-reach-understanding-coordinates-route-through-hormuz-iran-ministry-says-2026-08-05/)

### 2. Senate committee voted to hold Anthony Fauci in contempt

The committee approved a contempt resolution after Fauci invoked the Fifth Amendment during pandemic-response questioning. A committee vote does not itself create a conviction; the matter would ordinarily need Senate action before a referral. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/us-senate-panel-votes-hold-fauci-contempt-congress-2026-08-06/)

### 3. FDA approved the first orexin-targeting treatment for narcolepsy type 1

FDA approved Orzeyful (oveporexton) tablets for adults with narcolepsy type 1, the first treatment designed to restore orexin signaling and address the condition’s full symptom range. [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-drug-treat-full-range-narcolepsy-type-1-symptoms)

### 4. U.S. suspended government operations and avocado inspections in Michoacán after a threat

The suspension includes mandatory inspections needed for exports. Officials did not publicly specify the threat. [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/c5cf0ef4a260e6a517fae615c08a1f8b)

### 5. Overnight strikes killed civilians on both sides as a major Russian refinery burned

Ukrainian officials said Russian strikes killed six people; Russian officials said Ukrainian drones killed two civilians, while a Yaroslavl refinery burned. Claims were not independently verified. [Ukraine strikes](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/russian-strikes-kill-six-ukraines-balakliia-sumy-officials-say-2026-08-06/) · [Yaroslavl refinery](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-refinery-russias-yaroslavl-is-fire-after-ukrainian-drone-attack-governor-2026-08-06/)

## 2. Most Useful Development Today

### What the Orzeyful approval does—and does not mean

Approval is for adults with narcolepsy type 1 and rests on two 12-week placebo-controlled trials. It is a prescription treatment, not a cure; safety and individual suitability require clinician review. [FDA: evidence and safety](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-drug-treat-full-range-narcolepsy-type-1-symptoms)
