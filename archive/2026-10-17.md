## Top 5 Things to Know Today

### Trump waives nearly all new Graham Act sanctions and tariffs before they take effect

President Donald Trump signed an order Friday night waiving the broad Russia sanctions, financial restrictions and import duties Congress enacted last month, including tariffs aimed at major buyers of Russian oil and gas. The waivers remain until the president or designated officials decide further action is appropriate. The order says the pause is in the national interest but does not publish the report sent to Congress; sanctions already in force before the law remain in place. The move materially reduces the law's immediate pressure on Russia and countries financing its energy trade.

[Official White House order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/10/implementing-the-lindsey-o-graham-sanctioning-russia-and-iran-act-of-2026/) · [Public Law 119-111](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ111/html/PLAW-119publ111.htm) · [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/e50c561ab805883bb4e484c092d8fadd)

### Trudy holds at 90 mph as dangerous rain and coastal flooding threaten western Mexico

At 6 a.m. Central, the National Hurricane Center placed Category 1 Hurricane Trudy about 190 miles south-southeast of Manzanillo, moving northwest at 8 mph. The storm is expected to pass near southwestern and west-central Mexico later today into Sunday. Michoacán, Colima and Jalisco could receive 4–8 inches of rain, with isolated totals to 15 inches, along with hurricane conditions, surge, destructive waves, flash floods and landslides. Some strengthening is possible today before weakening Sunday; people in warning areas should follow Mexican authorities and current NHC updates.

[Official NHC public advisory 16A](https://prod-east-nhc.woc.noaa.gov/mobile/text/refresh/MIATCPEP1%2Bhtml/171143.shtml) · [Reuters companion](https://www.reuters.com/business/environment/hurricane-trudy-threatens-western-mexico-heavy-rain-coastal-flooding-2026-10-17/)

### Cost-of-living and student protests draw more than 30,000 people; police report 166 arrests

More than 140 demonstrations were recorded outside Paris by midday as labor and student groups protested prices, wages, pensions and education conditions. Most marchers were peaceful, but masked groups set fires and damaged property in Paris, Lyon and Marseille; police used tear gas and water cannons. The Interior Ministry reported at least 166 arrests. The mobilization links three weeks of school blockades with a broader attempt to revive the yellow-vest movement and is influencing France's presidential campaign.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/44b9d3ba91ffad4029f975dced7e31cb)

### CMS proposes accurate provider directories, out-of-network protections and a $0 generic copay for some dual eligibles

CMS issued a proposed rule for 2028 that would require Medicare Advantage plans to verify provider directories at least every 90 days, publish accuracy scores and cap a member's cost sharing at the in-network amount when an inaccurate listing led to out-of-network care. It would also implement a $0 generic-drug copay for full-benefit Medicare-Medicaid dual eligibles at or below 100% of the federal poverty level and impose new Pharmacy Benefit Manager reporting and compensation rules. These are proposals, not current benefits; the public-comment period begins with Federal Register publication.

[Official CMS fact sheet](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/medicare-program-implementation-sections-6219-6220-6224-consolidated-appropriations-act-2026-cms) · [Official CMS announcement](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-proposes-reforms-strengthen-transparency-affordability-medicare-advantage-part-d)

### Brazil reports the lowest annual Amazon deforestation since records began in 1988

Brazil's government said official satellite monitoring measured 3,335 square kilometers of Amazon forest loss from August 2025 through July 2026—the lowest annual rate in the series. The release is a major climate and biodiversity milestone after deforestation rose sharply under the prior administration, although thousands of square kilometers were still cleared and continued enforcement will determine whether the decline lasts.

[Associated Press via ABC News](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/brazil-amazon-deforestation-fell-lowest-level-1988-137329441)

## Supreme Court & Legal

### Justice Department and Lexington, Mississippi, agree to three years of police reforms

DOJ's 2024 investigation found a pattern of discriminatory policing against Black residents, excessive force, retaliatory arrests and unconstitutional fine-and-fee practices. The newly announced out-of-court agreement requires revised stop, search, arrest and force policies; changes to bail, release, fines and conflicts of interest; dismissal of unlawful warrants and expungement of certain fine-based convictions; training, reporting and three years of monitoring. The agreement resolves the federal pattern-or-practice matter without a trial, and only the parties—not private third parties—may enforce it.

[Official DOJ announcement](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-reaches-settlement-agreement-city-lexington-mississippi-regarding) · [Signed settlement agreement](https://www.justice.gov/crt/media/1465906/dl?inline=)

### Federal judge dismisses DOJ's challenge to New York City's sanctuary laws

The federal government asked a Brooklyn court to invalidate city rules limiting cooperation with civil immigration enforcement, including sharing release information and honoring ICE detainers without a judicial warrant. Judge Ramon Reyes dismissed the complaint for lack of a valid basis for relief and separately rejected the preemption and intergovernmental-immunity theories, reasoning that the federal government cannot commandeer local resources and that federal law does not require the requested custody information. This ends the district-court case; it is not a Supreme Court ruling, and DOJ may appeal to the Second Circuit.

[Readable Reuters report](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/us-judge-tosses-trump-administration-lawsuit-over-new-york-city-sanctuary-laws-4952774) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-judge-tosses-trump-administration-lawsuit-over-new-york-city-sanctuary-laws-2026-10-15/) · [Official DOJ complaint](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-sues-new-york-city-over-sanctuary-policies)

## Policy & Benefits

### CMS proposal targets directory errors, PBM compensation and low-income drug costs

For contract year 2028, CMS proposes 90-day provider-verification cycles, public directory accuracy scores and in-network cost sharing when a beneficiary reasonably relied on a wrong listing. It would require annual Pharmacy Benefit Manager compensation and cost-performance reporting, fair-market-value service payments and additional manufacturer-contract disclosures. A statutory change would reduce the generic copay from $1 to $0 for full-benefit dual eligibles at or below the poverty line. Final requirements may differ after public comment.

[Official CMS fact sheet](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/medicare-program-implementation-sections-6219-6220-6224-consolidated-appropriations-act-2026-cms)

### Cloudera accepts a two-year PERM debarment, $1.4 million back-pay fund and $600,000 penalty

DOJ and Labor resolved allegations that the data company denied qualified U.S. workers fair consideration for seven jobs advertised through the permanent-labor-certification process. Cloudera did not admit liability, but the settlement bars it from PERM sponsorship for two years, funds back pay, imposes civil penalties and requires revised recruiting procedures and monitoring. Potential claimants identified within 300 days may be eligible; DOJ's worker hotline is 1-800-255-7688.

[Official DOJ and Labor announcement](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/us-departments-justice-and-labor-secure-settlement-cloudera-enforce-two-year-perm-debarment) · [Settlement agreement](https://www.justice.gov/crt/media/1465571/dl)

The October 16 update added 330 cases from the prior week and says 96% of this year's confirmed cases are outbreak-associated. Forty-seven jurisdictions have reported resident cases, 42 new outbreaks have been recorded this year and kindergarten MMR coverage fell to 92.4% in 2025–26, below the roughly 95% community-immunity benchmark. ### New Free Journal Articles

#### [Identification of SGK1 as a candidate regulator associated with myeloid cell senescence in ME/CFS: An integrative transcriptomic and experimental study](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0359082)

PLOS One · online October 16, 2026 · reanalysis of human blood-transcriptomic datasets plus cell-line experiments. The discovery dataset included 8 men with post-infectious ME/CFS and 7 age-matched male controls; a single-cell analysis used 3 ME/CFS and 3 control samples, alongside aging reference samples. Computational screening highlighted SGK1 in monocytes and neutrophils, and knocking down SGK1 in chemically senesced THP-1 cells reduced several senescence-marker transcripts. This offers a hypothesis about immune aging in ME/CFS, not a diagnostic biomarker or available treatment. Major limitations are the tiny, male-only reused cohorts, selected single-cell samples, an artificial cell model and no validation in primary patient immune cells.

[Free full text (PLOS One)](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0359082) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42886747/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0359082)

#### [Late enhancement and washout maps for distinguishing tumor progression from radiation necrosis in malignant brain tumors: an exploratory study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12885-026-17170-0)

BMC Cancer · online October 15, 2026 · single-center retrospective imaging study of 33 treated patients: 25 with glioblastoma, 4 with high-grade astrocytoma, 1 with CNS lymphoma and 3 with brain metastases. Mean MRI washout ratios were 31% in recurrent disease, 35% in mixed pathology and 10% in radiation necrosis, suggesting the map may complement perfusion and diffusion imaging when scans are ambiguous. This matters because recurrence and treatment injury can require very different management, but it is not a validated stand-alone test. Limitations include only 6 radiation-necrosis cases, mixed tumor types, imaging rather than tissue confirmation in 13 patients, and no independent validation cohort.

[Free full text (BMC Cancer)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12885-026-17170-0) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42881455/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12885-026-17170-0)

#### [RSV vaccine uptake among Medicare Advantage enrollees: August 2023 to December 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13671429/)

Human Vaccines & Immunotherapeutics · online October 14, 2026 · observational analysis of Humana Medicare Advantage claims. Among approximately 1.7 million continuously enrolled adults who met the 2024 CDC recommendation, slightly more than 80% had no recorded RSV vaccination by December 31, 2024; uptake was higher at age 75+ and among people with higher-risk conditions. This identifies a prevention and outreach gap, not vaccine effectiveness or causation. Limitations include one insurer, adults 65+ only, claims that may miss outside vaccinations, incomplete capture of the 2024–25 season and author affiliations with Humana and Pfizer.

[Free full text (PubMed Central)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13671429/) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42872881/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/21645515.2026.2745402)
