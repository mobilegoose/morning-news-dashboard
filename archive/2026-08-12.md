## 1. Top 5 Things to Know Today

### 1. U.S. inflation eased slightly, but energy remained a major pressure

The Labor Department reported that consumer prices rose 0.1% in July and 3.4% over 12 months, down from 3.5% in June. Core inflation was 2.5%, while energy prices were still 14.7% higher than a year earlier. **Why it matters:** the report gives households modest relief and may reduce pressure for an immediate Federal Reserve rate increase, but inflation remains above the Fed's 2% target. ### 2. Colombia's earthquake rescue entered a critical phase

Authorities said the magnitude-7.4 earthquake had killed at least 181 people and injured 2,595; 195 were officially missing, while civilian lists were much larger. Crews searched overnight in Cali, Pereira, Manizales and Quibdó. **Why it matters:** survival odds fall sharply as time passes, and the large gap between official and civilian missing-person counts shows the continuing uncertainty. [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/colombia-earthquake-aid-search-survivors-a35440647cc3efed1bbce935d223565e)

### 3. Ukraine struck Russia's main Black Sea naval hub

Ukraine said anti-ship missiles plus aerial and sea drones damaged port infrastructure, air defenses and piers at Novorossiysk. Russian officials reported civilian casualties and damage to grain and industrial facilities. **Why it matters:** the operation expands Ukraine's pressure on a strategically important naval and export center, while also raising risks to civilians and global commodity flows. [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-black-sea-attack-9eadff0d7590923e3d98c99e2b61150b)

### 4. Medicaid and CHIP will stop federal payment for specified gender-affirming care for minors

The administration finalized a rule barring federal Medicaid and CHIP funding for puberty blockers, hormone therapy and surgeries for people under 19, with an October 13 effective date and a six-month transition for some existing hormone treatment. Mental-health services remain covered. **Why it matters:** the rule directly changes access for low-income transgender young people; states and families may face new costs, and legal challenges are expected. [Readable AP report](https://apnews.com/article/39722b01ab47a891bbca643a7ae5dde6) · [Reuters — access may be limited](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/us-bars-federal-funding-gender-affirming-care-low-income-minors-2026-08-12/)

### 5. The confirmed death toll from the Houthi ship attack rose to six

Pakistan said three of its citizens were among six people killed in the missile strike near Yemen's Bab el-Mandeb. The deaths were the first confirmed fatalities from Houthi maritime attacks in the current regional conflict. **Why it matters:** with the Strait of Hormuz disrupted, escalation on this alternative oil and trade route compounds risks to shipping, crews and energy prices. ## 2. Most Useful Development Today

### Most U.S. small businesses no longer have to file beneficial-ownership reports

FinCEN issued a final rule permanently exempting U.S. companies and U.S. persons from Corporate Transparency Act beneficial-ownership reporting. It will also delete previously submitted information that it reasonably believes belongs to U.S. persons. Foreign reporting companies still must report qualifying foreign beneficial owners. **Practical takeaway:** a domestic small business should not pay a service claiming it must file a new FinCEN ownership report under the old rules; verify any request against FinCEN's current FAQ. [Treasury announcement and rule summary](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0603)

### New Free Journal Articles

#### Calciphylaxis in chronic kidney disease: a systematic review of pathomechanisms and risk factors

**Journal and date:** *Renal Failure*, online August 10, 2026. **Evidence label:** peer-reviewed systematic review of human observational evidence. Researchers screened 2,374 publications and included 35 studies comparing people with chronic kidney disease who did and did not develop calcific uremic arteriolopathy. Low albumin, higher alkaline phosphatase, diabetes, higher body-mass index, female sex, vitamin-K-antagonist use and higher calcium-phosphate product were among factors that appeared consistently associated with the condition. **Why it matters:** calciphylaxis is rare, painful and often life-threatening; the synthesis may improve risk recognition in dialysis and advanced kidney disease. **Limitation:** the included studies were heterogeneous and largely observational, so the review identifies associations rather than proving that any factor causes calciphylaxis or that changing it prevents disease; the authors also found no standardized skin-biopsy evaluation system. [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13459315/) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42572798/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/0886022X.2026.2710397)
