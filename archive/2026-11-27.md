## Top 5 Things to Know Today

### [FAO and WFP add three El Niño-driven regions to the world’s hunger hotspots](https://www.fao.org/newsroom/detail/el-nino-adds-new-hunger-hotspots-as-conflict-continues-to-drive-risk-of-famine-warn-fao-and-wfp/en)

*1 · WORLD FOOD SECURITY · REPORT RELEASED TODAY*

A new FAO–World Food Programme early-warning report identifies 16 places where acute food insecurity is projected to worsen from December 2026 through May 2027, up from 13 in June. Southern Africa, the Central American Dry Corridor and northeastern Uganda were added largely because of El Niño; Chad was added because of spillover from Sudan. Sudan, South Sudan, Somalia, northern Nigeria and Yemen remain at the highest level of concern, with famine risk in parts of Sudan and South Sudan. The report is a forward-looking risk assessment, not a finding that famine exists throughout every listed country.

[FAO/WFP release](https://www.fao.org/newsroom/detail/el-nino-adds-new-hunger-hotspots-as-conflict-continues-to-drive-risk-of-famine-warn-fao-and-wfp/en) · [Full report page](https://www.wfp.org/publications/hunger-hotspots-fao-wfp-early-warnings-acute-food-insecurity)

### [EU leaders leave Ukraine financing unresolved after overnight budget talks](https://apnews.com/article/fd025046850b824b2e62def18408c6b6)

*2 · EUROPE · UKRAINE FINANCING · NO AGREEMENT*

European Union leaders ended roughly 12 hours of talks without agreement on the bloc’s next seven-year budget or a proposed €42 billion loan for Ukraine. The impasse leaves Kyiv’s 2027 financing plan unsettled and sends leaders back to negotiations in about three weeks. This is a concrete failure to reach agreement—not a prediction that the loan will ultimately fail.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/fd025046850b824b2e62def18408c6b6)

### [New Zealand’s final election count leaves a new centrist party holding the balance](https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/2026-general-election-official-results)

*3 · NEW ZEALAND · OFFICIAL ELECTION RESULT*

The Electoral Commission’s final count left neither the incumbent center-right bloc nor the center-left opposition with a parliamentary majority. The Opportunity Party can now determine which side can form a government through coalition or confidence-and-supply negotiations; if no governing agreement is reached, another election would be required. The official result changed the bargaining position—it did not itself install a government.

[Official final result](https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/2026-general-election-official-results) · [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/a19a838d8256a0762703ff0572aca3e6)

### [Presidential memorandum directs broader federal pursuit of capital cases](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/11/expeditious-enforcement-of-capital-sentences-and-the-pursuit-of-justice-for-victims-of-heinous-crimes/)

*4 · CRIMINAL JUSTICE · FEDERAL DEATH PENALTY · PUBLISHED TODAY*

A memorandum to the Attorney General directs DOJ, when lawful and appropriate, to pursue federal jurisdiction and seek the death penalty in specified murders of minors and murders committed during sexual abuse, child exploitation, sex trafficking, kidnapping or human trafficking. It also directs DOJ to prioritize direct appeals and post-conviction proceedings in existing federal capital cases. The memorandum changes executive-branch enforcement priorities; it does not create new capital crimes, eliminate prosecutorial judgment or displace defendants’ statutory and constitutional rights.

[White House memorandum](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/11/expeditious-enforcement-of-capital-sentences-and-the-pursuit-of-justice-for-victims-of-heinous-crimes/) · [Federal Register PDF](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-27/pdf/2026-24236.pdf)

### [NHTSA proposes a two-year delay for stronger automatic-braking requirements](https://www.nhtsa.gov/press-releases/trumps-transportation-department-extends-automatic-emergency-braking-mandate-two)

*5 · VEHICLE SAFETY · PROPOSED RULE · COMMENT PERIOD*

NHTSA’s proposal would move the compliance date for major automakers from September 1, 2029, to September 1, 2031, and for small-volume and final-stage manufacturers to September 1, 2032. It also proposes test tolerances and malfunction-warning procedures after agency testing found problems in high-speed and low-light scenarios. This is not yet a final delay: the existing standard remains in place while NHTSA accepts comments for 60 days after Federal Register publication under docket NHTSA-2026-2476.

[Official NHTSA proposal summary](https://www.nhtsa.gov/press-releases/trumps-transportation-department-extends-automatic-emergency-braking-mandate-two) · [Rulemaking docket](https://www.regulations.gov/docket/NHTSA-2026-2476)

## AI Today

*One substantive recent research release*

### [OpenAI reports early protein-design capability in GPT-6, but specialized models still lead](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities/)

*RECENT FALLBACK · AI AND BIOLOGY · NOVEMBER 24 · PREPRINT*

OpenAI tested GPT-6 on designing small protein binders against five targets. Of 322 synthesized designs, 35 showed binding in experimental assays (10.9%), indicating a general-purpose model can contribute useful candidates. The work is an early preprint, not a treatment study: specialized models were still used to propose or filter sequences, and GPT-6’s global fold-prediction performance remained far behind purpose-built systems. The result is best read as a research capability signal, not evidence of a clinically available therapy.

[OpenAI research summary](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities/) · [Preprint PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/gpt-6-a-universal-foundation-model-for-protein-biology.pdf)

*Two nonduplicative appellate arguments; both are recent fallbacks*

### [“Doe v. Blanche, No. 26-5238” — D.C. Circuit oral-argument audio](https://invisiblenetwork.ai/podcast/1067649051/12419fdcf74c)

*RECENT FALLBACK · LEGAL AUDIO · RELEASED NOVEMBER 24*

Transgender women in federal custody argue that transfer to men’s prisons would expose them to serious safety and medical risks in violation of the Eighth Amendment. After the D.C. Circuit vacated a categorical preliminary injunction and required individualized findings, the district court entered renewed relief for the named plaintiffs; the government is asking the appeals court to undo that injunction. The panel heard argument November 20 and has not issued a decision, so the recording explains an active appeal rather than a final ruling. It is useful for civil-rights, prison-safety and preliminary-injunction doctrine.

[Exact episode page](https://invisiblenetwork.ai/podcast/1067649051/12419fdcf74c) · [Official D.C. Circuit audio MP3](https://media.cadc.uscourts.gov/recordings/docs/2026/11/26-5238.mp3) · [Case background](https://www.nclrights.org/our-work/cases/doe-v-bondi/)

### [“Moss v. Cleo AI, No. 25-5856” — Ninth Circuit oral-argument audio](https://invisiblenetwork.ai/podcast/1067649051/1ae4cc56de3b)

*RECENT FALLBACK · LEGAL AUDIO · RELEASED NOVEMBER 24*

Active-duty servicemembers allege that Cleo’s cash advances and fees are consumer credit covered by the Military Lending Act, which caps costs and restricts mandatory arbitration. Cleo is appealing the district court’s refusal to dismiss the suit and compel arbitration, arguing that its advances do not trigger the statute. The Ninth Circuit heard argument November 16 and has not decided the appeal; the recording is useful for military consumer protection, fintech classification and arbitration enforceability.
