## Top 5 Things to Know Today

### Lula wins Brazil's presidential runoff and a fourth nonconsecutive term

With all ballots counted, President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva won Sunday's runoff with 51.8%—a lead of nearly 4.4 million votes. Flávio Bolsonaro rejected the result and sought annulment over a two-hour voting extension that both campaigns requested; the electoral court said the extension did not change the outcome. Lula is due to begin the new term January 5, 2027, making the result a major democratic and regional-policy development.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/brazil-election-lula-bolsonaro-runoff-vote-president-e2c9fe8fc4aa3e3675b9a8ec15405d93)

### Student-backed movement wins Serbia's election after 14 years of Vučić rule

With nearly 99% of ballots counted, the student-backed list won 52.7% and 139 of parliament's 250 seats; President Aleksandar Vučić conceded. The movement grew from protests after the Novi Sad station-canopy collapse killed 16 people. It now has a governing majority but has not selected a prime minister, so the transition and its effect on Serbia's EU path and ties with Russia remain open.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/26/europe-serbia-election-celebration-aleksandar-vucic-defeat-student-movement) · [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/fdf5def8a20aebc8ae139fd719478775)

### Regional officials converge on Tehran for de-escalation talks

Saudi Arabia's defense minister, Pakistan's army chief and Turkey's intelligence chief were in Tehran Monday, with Qatar also mediating, as the U.S.-Israel-Iran war and Houthi attacks strain the region. The meetings are a concrete diplomatic push, not a ceasefire agreement; fighting and election-related tension continue. Their importance lies in whether regional powers can establish channels that reduce further attacks and protect civilian infrastructure.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/iran-israel-us-war-10-26-2026-36486edb61b3bf4e0df67e8a17123e44)

### National victimization survey records a sharp 2025 drop in serious violence

The Justice Department's preliminary National Crime Victimization Survey estimates that nonfatal serious violence, excluding simple assault, fell 25% to 7.4 victimizations per 1,000 people in 2025; aggravated assault fell 31% and property victimization 19%. The survey covers reported and unreported crime through victim interviews. Because the redesigned instrument is not comparable with the pre-2024 legacy series and the results are preliminary, the release supports a recent decline but not a clean long-run comparison or a causal claim.

[Justice Department release](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/national-crime-victimization-survey-shows-violent-crime-plummeted-2025) · [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/violent-crime-victimization-2025-survey-data/)

### Anthropic files publicly for a Nasdaq listing

Anthropic filed its S-1 registration statement and plans to trade under the symbol ANT, bringing one of the largest AI developers closer to an initial public offering. The filing reports a first-half 2026 net loss of $80.7 billion, much of it tied to accounting for convertible notes, while the company has not yet set a share count, price range or final valuation. The filing is consequential for AI financing, but an offering is not guaranteed until the process is completed.

[Official Anthropic announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/s1-sec) · [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/anthropic-ipo-shares-public-ca2f4c282bef3201a392e59e3df2300c)


## Most Useful Development Today

### Check who operates a remote-monitoring program before Medicare's proposed payment change

A proposed CMS rule would stop traditional Medicare reimbursement when an outside health-technology vendor—not the clinician or facility's own employee—provides remote patient-monitoring services. It is a proposal, does not directly rewrite Medicare Advantage contracts and should not prompt anyone to stop using a prescribed device. If you rely on monitoring, ask whether the program is vendor-run, whether you have traditional Medicare, and what the practice's contingency is if the rule becomes final.

[KFF Health News — exact development](https://kffhealthnews.org/medicare/remote-patient-monitoring-cms-medicare-rule-reimbursements-access-rural-health/)


## U.S. & World

### Government and party monitors prepare for close midterm contests

The Justice Department plans to deploy roughly 1,000 election monitors across every state, while Democratic state parties, Common Cause and the Republican National Committee have trained much larger volunteer networks. DOJ says its contingent will not include armed or immigration-enforcement agents. Monitoring can document problems and deter interference, but competing legal teams may also intensify post-election disputes in close races.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/midterms-election-monitors-justice-department-democrats-republicans-4d36075a4f3d255d5f616778da206059)

### Seafood-plant fire forces evacuations over an ammonia risk

A fire at Pacific Seafood's Newport plant prompted a one-mile evacuation Sunday because of an ammonia tank; officials later reduced the zone to nearby blocks after the tank remained stable. Thirty to 40 people were on site, one firefighter was hospitalized for exhaustion and no other injuries were reported. The cause remained unknown while crews continued suppression, making this a late-yesterday developing safety event.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/newport-oregon-fire-evacuation-942a1c72ce7be8f36525be215403538a)


## Supreme Court & Legal

### Ninth Circuit questions DOJ's bid to segregate about 20,000 ballots

The Justice Department is asking the Ninth Circuit for emergency relief requiring California to segregate ballots tied to federal lists of registrations it says may involve noncitizens. At Sunday's hearing, two judges questioned federal jurisdiction and the government's Elections Clause theory; the panel had not ruled by the cutoff, and a district-court hearing is set for Tuesday. This is an emergency, interlocutory request—not a merits judgment and not proof that the listed voters are ineligible.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/15f4270b4c43b088a633c8fcc365d027)

### En banc Fifth Circuit rejects bond hearings for two longtime residents

In Sosnava Rodriguez v. Vergara, two longtime U.S. residents detained during removal proceedings sought individualized hearings at which the government would have to justify continued detention based on danger or flight risk. By 10–7, the full Fifth Circuit reversed habeas relief and held that due process does not require those hearings beyond what the statute provides. The ruling binds Texas, Louisiana and Mississippi; it is a merits appellate decision, and the Supreme Court has separately agreed to review the statutory question in related litigation.

[Fifth Circuit opinion](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca5/26-50183/26-50183-2026-10-23.html) · [Texas Tribune explainer](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/10/23/immigration-bond-detention-texas-court-ice-ruling-5th-circuit/?campaign=814407)

## Policy & Benefits

### CMS proposes upfront verification before subsidized Exchange coverage

CMS proposed requiring eligibility verification before advance premium tax credits or cost-sharing reductions begin, starting in 2028. The change would affect people seeking financial help, not enrollment without subsidies, and remains open to comment rather than being a current open-enrollment rule. If finalized, it could reduce improper payments but also create a documentation barrier for eligible applicants.

[Official CMS proposal summary](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-proposes-new-verification-requirements-implement-law-combats-exchange-fraud-protects-taxpayer)

### CMS opens a $275 million nursing-workforce initiative

The new national program offers eligible registered and licensed practical nurses up to $40,000 in loan repayment and/or a $10,000 stipend for three years of service at qualifying rural or high-shortage nursing homes. A first 90-day exception window runs October 26 through January 24 for eligible recent hires dating to July 28. The initiative may help staffing, but awards depend on eligibility, participating facilities and available funds.

[Official HHS/CMS announcement](https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/cms-launches-275-million-nurses-nursing-homes-initiative.html)

### New Medi-Cal asset limit will sharply tighten eligibility for older and disabled adults

Under policy enacted in June, the countable-asset limit for affected low-income seniors and disabled people will fall from $130,000 to $21,000 in July 2027. A primary home and one vehicle are generally exempt, while cash and some other assets count. Because transfers and spend-down decisions can have serious consequences, affected households should seek qualified benefits or special-needs planning advice rather than act from headlines alone.

[Associated Press / CalMatters](https://apnews.com/article/health-benefits-cuts-california-7d0accf339933558327fe3f964a5053a)


## Science, Medicine & Public Health

### U.S. measles total reaches 4,588 confirmed cases

CDC's latest update reports 4,588 confirmed 2026 cases, 43 outbreaks and 96% of cases associated with outbreaks—178 more cases than the prior weekly update. The count is more than double the 2,289 cases reported for all of 2025. This is a surveillance update rather than a forecast; local exposure notices and vaccination guidance remain more actionable than the national total alone.

[CDC measles data](https://www.cdc.gov/measles/data-research/index.html)

### Personalized mRNA treatment lowers recurrence risk in high-risk melanoma trial

In a randomized phase 3 study of more than 1,100 people whose high-risk melanoma had been surgically removed, Moderna's personalized intismeran plus Keytruda reduced the relative risk of recurrence or death by 42% versus Keytruda plus placebo after a median 24.6 months; two-year recurrence-free survival was 82% versus 70%. This is promising human trial evidence, but intismeran is experimental, overall-survival follow-up is immature, and approval, price and access remain unresolved.

[Official Moderna results](https://www.modernatx.com/recapping-the-phase-three-interpath-results) · [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/moderna-cancer-vaccine-melanoma-mrna-intismeran-415ececd79c9411498751246742aafa2)
