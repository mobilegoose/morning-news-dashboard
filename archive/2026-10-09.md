## Top 5 Things to Know Today

### A Category 3 hurricane bears down on the northern Gulf Coast

Hurricane Isaias strengthened to 120 mph as it approached a late-Friday landfall near the Alabama–Florida line. The National Hurricane Center warned of life-threatening storm surge, destructive wind, flash flooding and tornadoes; coastal evacuations and thousands of National Guard deployments were underway. People in the warning area should follow local emergency instructions now rather than rely only on the forecast center line.

[National Hurricane Center advisory archive](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/archive/2026/ISAIAS.shtml) · [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/919a46d4bd705726b4e57f18ce677ac5)

### White House creates a formal process that could lead to Lisa Cook’s removal

President Trump established a committee to investigate allegations that Federal Reserve Governor Lisa Cook made false statements on mortgage documents, with a closed hearing set for November 5. Cook denies wrongdoing and has not been charged. The step matters because it revives an unprecedented effort to remove a sitting Fed governor after the Supreme Court required notice and an opportunity to respond.

[White House memorandum](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/10/establishment-of-a-committee-of-inquiry-to-investigate-allegations-of-false-statements-by-lisa-denell-cook/) · [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/debef61d38fbb6a74e84a57fe8e26fc5)

### Chief Justice temporarily pauses an FCC deadline in an election-ad dispute

In Federal Communications Commission v. Brown, the FCC and Republican committees asked the Supreme Court to stop a Fourth Circuit order requiring an immediate agency response to Democrats’ challenge to discounted political-ad rules. Chief Justice Roberts issued an administrative stay and required a response by October 10. That short pause preserves the status quo while the stay request is considered; it is not a merits ruling on the FCC policy.

[Official Supreme Court docket](https://www.supremecourt.gov/docket/docketfiles/html/public/26A476.html) · [Case-specific tracker](https://www.supremecourtcasetracker.com/emergency-docket/26a476)

### Labor Department freezes PERM processing for major tech employers

The administration suspended new and pending permanent-labor-certification processing for Microsoft, Adobe, Cognizant, Infosys, Tata Consultancy Services, Wipro, HCL and Capgemini while alleging program abuse; it also opened exchange-visitor inquiries involving nine universities. A PERM suspension can interrupt a common employment-based green-card path, but it is not the same as canceling every worker’s existing visa or status.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/e7b3a407f822702b269ee277d21343ea) · [Reuters report (potentially limited)](https://www.reuters.com/business/us-suspending-permanent-residency-program-for-microsoft-vance-says-2026-10-08/)

### Riyadh airport attacks kill three and disrupt more than 120 flights

Saudi authorities said Houthi-claimed attacks on King Khalid International Airport and a Saudia aircraft killed three Saudi citizens, including a pilot, and wounded several people. At least 124 Friday flights were canceled and the Saudi-led coalition struck Houthi positions in Sanaa. The exchange expands the Iran-linked regional conflict and raises fresh risk for civilian aviation and Red Sea trade routes.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/5226e214e7c50ceff68915379ec3a714)

## Supreme Court & Legal

### Administrative stay postpones an agency deadline but decides no one’s legal claim

Democratic candidates and committees asked the FCC to reject a revised policy that lets national party committees obtain broadcasters’ lowest unit rates for certain coordinated ads; after the agency did not act, the Fourth Circuit ordered a response by October 9. The FCC and Republican committees sought emergency Supreme Court relief. Chief Justice Roberts granted only an administrative stay—temporarily lifting the deadline—and requested the challengers’ response by 5 p.m. October 10. The full stay application remains pending, the policy’s legality is unresolved, and the lower-court matter can resume if the pause ends.

[Official docket 26A476](https://www.supremecourt.gov/docket/docketfiles/html/public/26A476.html) · [Fourth Circuit order](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca4/26-2230/26-2230-2026-10-07.html) · [Case-specific explainer](https://www.supremecourtcasetracker.com/emergency-docket/26a476)

### A new White House inquiry attempts to supply the process the Court required

President Trump previously tried to remove Fed Governor Lisa Cook based on mortgage allegations; Cook challenged the removal and asked to remain in office. On June 29, the Supreme Court denied the administration’s stay application after argument, leaving Cook in place and holding that she needed notice and an opportunity to respond before removal. That was not a finding that the allegations were true or false, nor a final resolution of every lower-court issue. The new committee will present evidence, hold a November 5 hearing and recommend whether statutory “cause” exists; any removal could produce new litigation.

[Official docket and June 29 disposition](https://www.supremecourt.gov/docket/docketfiles/html/public/25A312.html) · [White House memorandum](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/10/establishment-of-a-committee-of-inquiry-to-investigate-allegations-of-false-statements-by-lisa-denell-cook/) · [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/debef61d38fbb6a74e84a57fe8e26fc5)

DOJ filed a complaint challenging a Denver ordinance that restricts masks and prescribes identification for federal law-enforcement officers. The department argues the city cannot regulate federal operations under the Supremacy Clause and says the rules endanger officers. These are allegations at the filing stage; the court has not ruled on validity or enforcement.

## Policy & Benefits

### PERM freeze creates immediate uncertainty for workers at eight employers

Permanent labor certification is normally the employer’s first step in many employment-based green-card cases. A processing freeze can stall a case and may interact with workers’ separate visa timelines, but effects depend on the person’s status, priority date and filing stage. Affected workers should preserve every notice and seek case-specific advice rather than assume an existing visa was automatically revoked.

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/e7b3a407f822702b269ee277d21343ea) · [Reuters report (potentially limited)](https://www.reuters.com/business/us-suspending-permanent-residency-program-for-microsoft-vance-says-2026-10-08/)

### WHO publishes an implementation guide for moving long-stay patients out of institutions

The new guide tells psychiatric hospitals how to reduce admissions and long stays, transform inpatient practice and coordinate supported discharge with housing, social, education and employment services. WHO says only 9% of countries report a full shift to community-based care. The guide is operational advice rather than a U.S. entitlement change, but it is directly relevant to disability rights, independent living and service design.

[WHO implementation guide](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240124134) · [WHO announcement](https://www.who.int/singapore/news/detail-global/09-10-2026-who-urges-shift-from-institutional-to-community-based-mental-health-care)

### HHS opens a clinic and proposes paying clinicians for suspected-injury reports

HHS said NIH opened a clinic October 5 for people reporting health problems after vaccination; CMS proposed reimbursing clinician time spent submitting suspected injuries to VAERS; and federal agencies are modernizing reporting and electronic-record standards. These actions expand care and evidence collection. A VAERS report is a safety signal and does not by itself establish that a vaccine caused an event.

[HHS announcement](https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/hhs-strengthens-vaccine-injury-research-reporting-patient-care.html)

### Cleo refund distribution begins without a claim fee

The FTC’s payment process covers more than 2.1 million customers it says were misled about cash-advance amounts and timing. Eligible people should use the instructions in the official notice, redeem payments promptly and report impersonators; the FTC says no one must pay to receive a refund.

[FTC refund announcement](https://search.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/10/ftc-returns-more-158-million-consumers-misled-cash-advance-app-company-cleo-ai)

## Science, Medicine & Public Health

### Federal vaccine-safety initiative links patient care, reporting and research

The HHS package creates a clinical route for selected patients and seeks more complete adverse-event data through clinician reimbursement, modernized VAERS tools and electronic-health-record fields. Better reporting can identify patterns worth testing, but passive reports remain vulnerable to missing information, reporting bias and coincidental events; causal conclusions require controlled investigation.

[HHS announcement](https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/hhs-strengthens-vaccine-injury-research-reporting-patient-care.html)

### New Free Journal Articles

#### [English primary-care records show a 2022 rise in coded pediatric Long COVID and post-viral fatigue](https://bjgpopen.org/content/early/2026/10/08/BJGPO.2026.0163)

BJGP Open · online October 8, 2026 · retrospective population-based dynamic cohort using CPRD Aurum records for 7,442,572 children under 18, including 3,624,692 during the pandemic. Researchers identified 6,700 Long COVID consultations and 20,440 post-viral-fatigue consultations; coded Long COVID peaked at 140.3 per 100,000 child-years in 2022, with higher rates among older children, girls and those in less-deprived areas. Why it matters: it quantifies recognized illness in routine care, but not true population prevalence. Limitations include undercoding, new codes introduced only in November 2020, possible misclassification, about 50% missing ethnicity and differences in care-seeking and clinician awareness. This observational record study shows associations and documentation trends, not causation or treatment effectiveness.

Google Cloud announced an agent that can answer questions, create media, write and run code, act across Workspace and business systems, and choose among models. The company emphasized per-agent identities, least-privilege permissions, audit trails, sandboxing and cost controls. Availability and results will vary by product and customer configuration, and the performance claims are Google’s own.

OpenAI reported banning two influence operations that used its models alongside fake journalist personas, fabricated documents and co-opted organizations to place geopolitical content in real outlets. This is a platform investigation, not a judicial finding, but the disclosed tactics are useful for editors and researchers assessing provenance.
