New Free Journal Articles · Observational cohort

Long-term angiogenic and thromboinflammatory signatures in post-COVID-19 syndrome

Angiogenesis · e-published Aug. 7. Thirty-two adults hospitalized with severe or critical COVID-19 were followed 3.5 years. Persistent symptoms and several vascular, inflammatory and coagulation abnormalities were observed; this association does not prove causation or establish treatment.

Why it matters: unusually long Long COVID follow-up. Limitation: small single-center cohort, no matched control group and limited generalizability.

New Free Journal Articles · Systematic review

Adoptive cellular therapies for pediatric brain tumors

Neurosurgical Review · e-published Aug. 7. Reviewers screened 221 unique records and extracted 34 preclinical and early clinical studies. Several targets and intracranial delivery strategies showed activity or feasibility, but clinical evidence remains small and early phase.

Why it matters: severe pediatric brain tumors have major unmet need. Limitation: heterogeneous evidence, much of it preclinical, without established efficacy.

Free full textPubMed

New Free Journal Articles · Retrospective comparison

Right- versus left-predominant frontotemporal dementia

Journal of Neurology · e-published Aug. 7. In 51 clinic patients, right-predominant cases more often showed person-recognition and behavioral changes, but overlap was substantial and differences narrowed over time.

Why it matters: cautions against classifying disability from imaging laterality alone. Limitation: retrospective specialist sample, modest diagnostic performance and no external validation.

Free full textPubMed
