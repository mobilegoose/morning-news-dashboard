3 · SUPREME COURT & IMMIGRATION

Court sends the Venezuelan TPS case back for reconsideration

In Mullin v. National TPS Alliance, Venezuelan TPS holders challenged DHS decisions that vacated an extension and ended their protection; the administration asked the Court to undo a Ninth Circuit ruling that the secretary exceeded statutory authority. The Court granted review, vacated that judgment and remanded for reconsideration under its June Mullin v. Doe ruling limiting judicial review of TPS termination decisions. This is a GVR, not a merits win after briefing: the Ninth Circuit must now rework the case under the new precedent, with protection and work authorization consequences still to be resolved.

4 · SUPREME COURT & CLIMATE

Justices hear whether federal law blocks Boulder’s climate-damages suit

Boulder city and county seek state-law damages from Suncor and Exxon, alleging the companies promoted fossil fuels and misled the public about climate harms; the companies ask the Court to stop the case because interstate and international emissions are governed by federal law. Today’s oral argument begins the merits process—it is not a ruling. The decision, expected by June 2027, could determine whether dozens of similar state and local suits proceed; Justice Alito is recused.

Official docketAssociated Press

5 · KOREAN PENINSULA

South Korea attributes DMZ blast that injured three soldiers to North Korean mines

A joint South Korean and U.N. Command investigation concluded that North Korean mines caused the September explosion just south of the military demarcation line. Seoul demanded an apology and removal of the mines; Pyongyang has denied responsibility. The finding raises the risk of escalation along an already heavily fortified border.

Supreme Court & Legal

Final same-morning court check included

WASHINGTON REDISTRICTING · GVR

Court orders a new look at challenges to Washington’s legislative map

Garcia v. Hobbs and Trevino v. Hobbs concern a court-drawn Yakima Valley district created after the state map was found to dilute Hispanic voting strength; voters and a legislator argued the remedy was unlawfully race-conscious and disputed who had standing to challenge it. The Court granted the petitions, vacated the Ninth Circuit judgments and remanded for reconsideration under Louisiana v. Callais. A GVR does not choose a winner or invalidate the map by itself; the lower court must apply the newer Voting Rights Act precedent.

VENEZUELAN TPS · GVR

National TPS Alliance returns to the Ninth Circuit under a narrower review rule

DHS asked the Court to overturn a ruling that its Venezuela TPS actions exceeded statutory authority. The justices granted review, vacated the judgment and remanded in light of Mullin v. Doe, which held that Congress barred judicial review of nonconstitutional challenges to TPS designation and termination decisions. The lower court now decides what claims, if any, remain; the order is not a fresh merits opinion.

Suncor v. Boulder opens the Court’s new term

Oil companies want the Court to hold that federal law precludes Boulder’s state-law deception and damages claims over global greenhouse-gas emissions. Boulder argues its consumer-protection and tort claims can proceed in state court. The Court heard argument today and will decide later; it also asked the parties to address whether it has jurisdiction at this interlocutory stage.

NEW FREE JOURNAL ARTICLE · SPINE BIOMECHANICS

Biomechanical, Structural, and Composition Characterization of the Vertebra-Disc Interface

JOR Spine · online October 2, 2026 · peer-reviewed human cadaver study. Researchers tested 45 lumbar vertebra-disc interface samples with MRI, micro-CT and bend/compression loading. Structural measures predicted mechanical behavior more consistently than age, disc degeneration or tissue composition, while fracture strain varied widely (1%–52%). Why it matters: the work may improve models of spinal failure and implant design. Limitation: cadaveric segments do not establish pain, disability, healing or outcomes in living patients, and the sample was modest.

NEW FREE JOURNAL ARTICLE · HYPOPITUITARISM

Functional analysis of ACTH-producing pituitary cells derived from human pluripotent stem cells

Stem Cell Research & Therapy · online October 2, 2026 · peer-reviewed preclinical animal study. Human stem-cell-derived pituitary-hypothalamus organoids transplanted under the skin of hypopituitary SCID mice survived for more than six months, increased ACTH and prolonged survival; one cynomolgus monkey showed short-term hormone function over six weeks. Why it matters: it is proof of concept for cell replacement in severe pituitary failure. Limitation: there is no human clinical evidence, and immune rejection, manufacturing, tumor risk, dosing and durability remain unresolved.
