2. Most Useful Development Today

Small-business compliance

FinCEN reporting

Most U.S. small businesses no longer have to file beneficial-ownership reports

FinCEN permanently exempted U.S. companies and U.S. persons from Corporate Transparency Act ownership reporting and will delete previously submitted information it reasonably believes belongs to U.S. persons. Foreign reporting companies retain narrower duties.

Practical takeaway A domestic small business should not pay a service claiming it must file under the old rules; verify any request against FinCEN's current guidance.

New Free Journal Articles · Systematic review

Calciphylaxis in chronic kidney disease: pathomechanisms and risk factors

Renal Failure · online August 10, 2026. This peer-reviewed review screened 2,374 publications and included 35 human observational studies comparing people with chronic kidney disease who did and did not develop calcific uremic arteriolopathy. Low albumin, higher alkaline phosphatase, diabetes, higher body-mass index, female sex, vitamin-K-antagonist use and higher calcium-phosphate product were consistently associated with the condition.

Why it matters Calciphylaxis is rare, painful and often life-threatening; the synthesis may improve risk recognition in dialysis and advanced kidney disease. Limitation: studies were heterogeneous and largely observational, so associations do not establish causation or prevention; no standardized skin-biopsy evaluation system was found.
