New Free Journal Articles · Randomized trial

Intraoperative nitrous oxide after cervical spinal-cord injury

Pain Reports · e-published Aug. 5. One center randomized 80 adults having emergency traumatic cervical-SCI surgery; 59 were analyzed. Nitrous oxide did not improve the primary outcome and day-one pain was higher (mean NRS 5.10 vs 3.27; p=.021). A lower six-month pain score was exploratory, not an established benefit.

Why it matters: neuropathic pain is a major functional burden. Limitation: single center, 21 exclusions after randomization and a secondary long-term result.

New Free Journal Articles · Retrospective analysis

Anti-amyloid eligibility in atypical Alzheimer phenotypes

Neurology · e-published Aug. 5. In 184 biomarker-confirmed patients, 70%–85% would be ineligible under major trial or appropriate-use frameworks, often because bedside score cutoffs excluded people with early symptomatic disease. This evaluates selection rules, not treatment effects.

Why it matters: rigid criteria may shape access for non-memory-first disability. Limitation: retrospective, single tertiary center and theoretical eligibility only.

Free full textPubMed

New Free Journal Articles · Exploratory diagnostic study

EEG classification of sporadic Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease

PLOS ONE · e-published Aug. 6. Researchers compared six people with sporadic CJD, 23 with Alzheimer disease and 10 healthy adults. Leakage-controlled nested validation reached 84.6%; the higher 97.4% figure came from the selected model.

Why it matters: sCJD is rapidly disabling, hard to diagnose early and on SSA’s CAL list. Limitation: only six cases, no external cohort and no clinical-utility test.
