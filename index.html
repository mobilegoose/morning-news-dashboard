New Free Journal Articles · Retrospective cohort

Genetic and pregnancy outcomes with increased fetal nuchal translucency

PLOS One · e-published Aug. 7. Researchers studied 180 pregnant women, selected from a 1,678-pregnancy cohort, using karyotyping and SNP arrays. Karyotyping found 24 chromosomal abnormalities; arrays found additional copy-number variants.

Why it matters: supports microarray as a complement to karyotyping and counseling. Limitations: retrospective single-center referral sample, varying cutoffs and limited follow-up; uncertain variants are not diagnoses.

New Free Journal Articles · Laboratory and translational study

LIMD1 loss and PD-L1 immune evasion in lung cancer

Life Science Alliance · e-published Aug. 7. Laboratory experiments linked LIMD1 loss to stabilized PD-L1 and reduced CD8 T-cell activation. In the 421-patient TRACERx cohort, clonal loss appeared in about 40% of lung adenocarcinomas and was associated with higher PD-L1.

Why it matters: a potential direction for checkpoint-therapy biomarker research. Limitations: much evidence is laboratory-based, patient analyses are observational, and no clinical test is validated.
