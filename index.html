Federal agents search Rep. Ilhan Omar’s home

Saudi Arabia investigates the air-defense failure behind the Riyadh airport attack

HURRICANE SIMON · MEXICO

Simon’s slow crawl keeps flooding, mudslide and storm-surge risks high

The National Hurricane Center placed Simon just offshore of Jalisco early Monday with 115 mph winds and a movement of only 2 mph. Slow motion can prolong destructive rain even as peak winds weaken. Anyone in or traveling to Jalisco, Colima, Nayarit, Sinaloa or Michoacán should follow local evacuation orders, avoid flooded roads and use the official advisory for changing warning zones rather than relying on an old forecast track.

SEARCH OF A MEMBER OF CONGRESS

What is known—and not known—about the Omar search

DOJ confirmed only that Homeland Security Investigations used a judicially authorized warrant in an ongoing criminal investigation. Officials did not disclose the suspected offense, the warrant materials, what was seized or whether Omar is a target; no charge has been announced. Those gaps are legally important because a probable-cause warrant authorizes a search, not a conviction or even necessarily a charge against the resident.

Science, Medicine & Public Health

LONG COVID · OBSERVATIONAL STUDY

Airway microbiota patterns track with Long COVID severity and symptom profiles

European Respiratory Journal · online October 11, 2026 · prospective multicenter PHOSP-COVID cohort of 771 adults previously hospitalized with COVID-19. Oral and sputum microbial profiles at six months differed across pulmonary, neurocognitive and gastrointestinal symptom groups, and some lactic-acid bacteria correlated with inflammatory proteins. These are associations, not proof that the microbes cause symptoms or that changing the microbiome would treat Long COVID; the cohort also does not represent people whose acute infection was never hospitalized.

RECENT FALLBACK · TINNITUS GUIDELINE

Updated guideline emphasizes CBT, hearing evaluation and self-harm screening

The American Academy of Otolaryngology–Head and Neck Surgery guideline for adults with persistent, bothersome tinnitus recommends targeted history and examination, comprehensive audiology, education, CBT and hearing-device evaluation when appropriate. It recommends against routine imaging without warning signs and against supplements, routine drug treatment solely for tinnitus and repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation. The guidance was released October 9 and newly indexed today; it excludes many secondary causes and is not a substitute for individual evaluation, especially for pulsatile, one-sided or neurologically accompanied symptoms.

NEW FREE JOURNAL ARTICLE · PEER-REVIEWED DIAGNOSTIC VALIDATION LETTER

Low-input MYD88/CD79B assay improves detection in rare eye and brain lymphomas

Experimental Hematology & Oncology · online October 9, 2026 · paired laboratory validation in 46 vitreous-fluid or cerebrospinal-fluid specimens. The multiplex PCR/Sanger workflow reported 81.8% sensitivity, 100% specificity and 94.7% accuracy while using much less DNA than the existing MYD88-only test and adding CD79B variants. Why it matters: fragile, cell-poor specimens make primary vitreoretinal and central-nervous-system lymphoma difficult to diagnose. Important limits: this was a small specimen study, not a treatment trial; estimates need broader external validation and a negative result cannot rule out disease.
